Every variant fit its own mask to the same tolerance: per-joint teacher-set error on supervised dims ≤ **0.021** for all three (M1 0.019/0.017; M2 0.020/0.009/0.018/0.007; M3 max 0.021, most ≤0.010) against a parent baseline error of 0.59–0.69 on hip yaw. The carrier did exactly its job: M1/M2 unsupervised joints stayed at the parent (divergence **0.0025 / 0.0029**) and far from the teacher (**0.146 / 0.147**) — which is precisely why they cannot turn.

**The covariate shift O2 measured was a *consequence* of the partial mask, not an independent problem.** Expression on the student's own states rises with mask size: M1 hip yaw **21% / 5%** of target; M2 hip yaw 25% / 6% but hip pitch **72% / 115%**; M3 **88–100% on all twelve joints** (only r_ankle_pitch lower at 68%). Supervising the whole action keeps the student on the teacher's state manifold, so the expression gap closes on its own — the DAgger round proposed in O2 for the {0,6} mask would have chased a symptom.
