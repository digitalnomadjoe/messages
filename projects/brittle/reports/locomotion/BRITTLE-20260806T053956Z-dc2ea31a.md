## 2. Dataset construction — and the design that had to be abandoned

**Abandoned (recorded, `o2_openloop_divergence_ABANDONED.py`):** harvesting observations by replaying the teacher's recorded actions open-loop on a second sim. Two *identical* replays of the same actions from the same state diverged to **max |Δqpos| 0.137 m and max |Δobs| 14.1** over 1400 steps — with no stabilising feedback, GPU-atomic contact nondeterminism amplifies. ★ **Open-loop action replay is not a valid long-horizon data source**; it also failed its own determinism gate, which is how it was caught.

**Adopted:** ONE trajectory, dual view. The era teacher runs closed-loop on the **current** stack (plant v2, walking-law v2, **deployed** hip-yaw scale 0.20) with the O1 D1 repair (obs[33:45] in era units, so the teacher is in-distribution). A **current adapter rides along as a passive observer** — it never steps or writes to the sim; each step it mirrors the phase/anchor bookkeeping and calls its own `_augment_obs`. Observation and teacher action therefore describe the **same state exactly**. Generating trajectory: **0/24 falls, TURNING_LEFT on all 8 graded worlds, κ 1.814 ± 0.077**, τP99 9.36, lift 29.1 mm.

## 4. The BC, and why it is insufficient

Bounded BC from L2-final: `L_turn` (dims {0,6} only) + `L_carrier` (other ten → frozen parent) + `L_retain` (all 12 → retention set), 2,000 steps, batch 1024, lr 3e-4, log_std untouched. Result: turn gap **0.998 → 0.0012 (−99.9%)**, carrier 1.1e-4, retention 3.4e-4. ★ Note the retention baseline is **exactly 0 by construction** (targets are the parent's own actions on its own states) — the first run's relative early-stop guard fired spuriously on a healthy run and was replaced with an absolute one.

**Liveness — the decisive measurement.** On the student's *own* native-rollout states with the turn command live (7,760 rows): the supervision is **live, not collapsed** (|student − parent| on hip-yaw = 0.205 / 0.105), but the student expresses only a fraction of the target — student hip-yaw mean **[−0.143, +0.030]** against the teacher target **[−0.672, +0.564]**, i.e. **≈21% and ≈5%** of it. A 99.9% fit on teacher states and ~20% expression on its own states is the textbook covariate-shift signature. Two contributing facts are also on the record: at the **deployed** 0.20 scale, **20.4% / 10.9%** of the hip-yaw targets are saturated at ±1 (they do not fit in the action box); and O1's necessity probe showed hip-yaw is necessary but not sufficient — removing it from the teacher drops κ 1.83 → 0.59, so the other ten dims carry the remainder, and grafting hip-yaw onto a straight carrier does not recover the whole-body synergy.
