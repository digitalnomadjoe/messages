## 0. The blocker, stated exactly

The ticket's binding requirement is:

> *"Do NOT achieve independence by pure temporal retiming. For every reference: `stride = v / cadence` must be reflected in the spatial gait geometry."*

**Measured against that standard, the repository can build two of the three axes and not the third.**

| axis | mechanism that exists | genuine spatial geometry? |
|---|---|---|
| **speed** at fixed cadence | `build_crown_command_family.stride_scale(e, ss)` — *"uniform stride reduction at FIXED cadence — path forward advance, BOTH hip-pitch deviations (mean-preserving) and the foot-rel fore-aft channel scale together"* | **YES** ✔ |
| **curvature** | `stance_twist` + `step_diff` (edit `jq` and foot-rel fore-aft) then the committed path-frame bend from `build_curved_walk_ref.py` | **YES** ✔ |
| **cadence** at fixed speed | `build_crown_command_family.py`, straight members: *"the cmd-phase clock is the retime mechanism — decision B — so **the motion arrays are identical across cadence members and only the command metadata differs**"* | **NO — metadata-only retime** ✘ |

★★ **The one capability the ticket forbids is the only one the generator has for cadence.** A cadence family built today would be 19 members whose joint trajectories are identical and whose `gait_cycle_hz` field differs — precisely the degenerate construction that produced the collapse measured in the preflight (`obs52` and `obs53` moving one shared stride latent). Generating it would bake the defect in and call it a fix.

**A geometric cadence axis is achievable — it exists.** `ccref4b` has one, and I verified it is not a retime: resampling each loop to a common 200-point phase grid (which removes timing entirely), the joint trajectories still differ by **0.070 rad** (c 0.88 vs 1.00) and **0.311 rad** (c 1.15 vs 1.00), and hip-pitch swing amplitude changes **0.769 → 0.737 → 0.410 rad**. A pure retime gives ~0 and identical amplitude.

⚠ **But `ccref4b` did not come from a generator.** Its provenance field reads `ccref4_source_tiled(frontier sA2-4M realized gait; cycle-avg+mirror-symmetrized)` — it was **sourced from a trained policy's realized gait**. The curved members in `family_q8d` likewise read `era 44aa372 p12t concat member 3 … slice [1862,2510)` — **extracted from a previous era's teacher reference**. Neither is reproducible at a new operating point, and the policy that would have to supply a realized gait at the new `(v, c, κ)` cells is the policy this family is meant to train.

**Inventory, to be unambiguous:** `ccref4b` = genuine `(v, c)` geometry at **v ∈ {0.24, 0.27, 0.29, 0.32}, zero curved variants**. `family_q8d` = curvature at the **single** point `(0.153, 1.00)`. **The intersection of "curved" and "non-nominal `(v, c)`" is empty, and so is the intersection of "geometric cadence" and "generatable".**

**I did not generate any member.** Producing 19 retimed-cadence members and reporting them as a speed/cadence family would have satisfied the letter of the deliverable and defeated its purpose.

## 1. What clears the blocker — one missing capability

**A cadence-parametric gait synthesizer**: given a base cycle and a target `(v, c)`, emit a reference whose *spatial* step geometry matches `stride = v/c` — i.e. the cadence analogue of the `stride_scale` that already exists for speed. Concretely it must change swing amplitude and step timing within the cycle, not just relabel `gait_cycle_hz`.

Three routes, cheapest first:

1. **Extend `stride_scale` to a cadence axis.** It already proves the pattern: scale path advance, hip-pitch deviation and foot-rel fore-aft together, channel-consistently. A cadence variant additionally re-times the *within-cycle* swing/stance split and re-scales swing amplitude to the new stride. Smallest change, reuses a committed and gate-covered mechanism. **My recommendation.**
2. **RGL curved/parametric walk export.** `Walking turn reference generation` and velocity-parameterised export are listed as RGL *goals*; the shipped headless exports are cycle / start-stop / TIP / march / standing — **there is no curved-walk or `(v,c)`-parametric walk export today**. Larger job, but it is the canonical reference path.
3. **Source realized gaits**, as `ccref4b` did — requires a policy that already walks at the target `(v, c, κ)`. Circular here.

## 2. Revised lattice — delivered, and it fixes a real error in my own previous design

⚠ **My 12U-C11S15SC lattice was wrong on the ticket's requirement 2.** I labelled the four box corners "held-out interpolation", but they lie **outside** the convex hull of the training points I proposed (training ran `(0.110,1.00) → (0.130,0.85)`, and `(0.110,0.85)` sits below-left of that edge). They were extrapolation probes mislabelled as interpolation. **Corrected below by making the corners training anchors**, so the hull is the whole box and every held-out point is a true interpolation.

**Command box:** `v ∈ [0.10, 0.24] m/s`, `c ∈ [0.85, 1.20] Hz`, `κ ∈ [−2.0, +2.0] rad/m`.

## 3. Advantage-normalisation partition — proved on the design

Per-member stratification (19 strata) is *safe* here but is not the principled choice. **Partition by command regime: κ-sign × cadence band = 9 strata.** At `nworld = 4032` round-robin over 19 members (212–213 worlds each) and 64 steps/world/iteration:

| stratum | members | worlds | samples/iter |
|---|---|---|---|
| κ=0 × c 0.85 | 4 | 848 | 54,272 |
| κ=0 × c 1.00 | 1 | 212 | **13,568** ← smallest |
| κ=0 × c 1.20 | 4 | 848 | 54,272 |
| κ=+2 × c 0.85 | 2 | 424 | 27,136 |
| κ=+2 × c 1.00 | 1 | 212 | 13,568 |
| κ=+2 × c 1.20 | 2 | 424 | 27,136 |
| κ=−2 × {0.85, 1.00, 1.20} | 2/1/2 | 424/212/424 | mirror |

**Smallest stratum = 13,568 samples/iteration, 212× the 64-sample minimum** below which `--adv-norm-per-regime` silently falls back to global statistics. **No stratum can trigger the fallback**, and speed stays continuous *within* strata so it is normalised jointly rather than shattered. Verified arithmetic on the design; not assumed from the old 3-regime setup.

## 4. Held-out qualification design

All 13 ticket criteria carried forward, with the decisive ones called out: **(3) cadence response sign correct** — today it is *inverted*, so sign is the primary gate, not tolerance; **(5) iso-stride commands must produce distinctly different realized speeds/cadences** with non-overlapping CI95 at 32×5 — a stride-only reader cannot pass this; **(7) curvature must not secretly force cadence toward ~1.5 Hz** — the preflight measured curvature dragging cadence +39 %, so this is a real failure mode with a measured baseline. Torque is monitoring (per-joint P99 and 1 s-RMS P99 against the Part-A baseline); max and saturation duration remain diagnostic.

## 5. Smallest recommended next unit

**Not PPO, and not reference generation either.** It is a **bounded cadence-geometry mechanism unit**: extend `stride_scale` to a cadence axis, and prove on *two* members at fixed speed that (i) the zero-change identity holds, (ii) the phase-resampled joint trajectories differ like `ccref4b`'s do (≥ ~0.07 rad, swing amplitude tracking stride), and (iii) the existing reference gates still pass. One mechanism, two members, no training. Only once that is green can the 19-member family be generated and gated, and only then can PPO be scoped.
