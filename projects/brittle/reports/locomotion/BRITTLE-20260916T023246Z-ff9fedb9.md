## 1. The seated pose (declared here — none existed) and why it is the only floor-sit

BRITTLE had no seated pose, family or predicate. The knee is BD-X-like: `knee_pitch` ∈ [−155°, 0°] and negative knee swings the shank **forward/up** of the thigh line (backward-bending), so a human sit — shins down in front of the pelvis — is kinematically impossible with the soles on the floor. The only floor sit with flat soles is microduck's belly-sit: **pelvis resting on its bottom face** (root z = 57.7 mm, the pelvis box half-height; upright spec quaternion; yaw 0), **legs symmetric, folded back** (thigh up-and-back, shank down-and-back, feet under/behind the pelvis), **soles flat** (ankle = −(hip + knee)), the knee solved per hip so the sole spheres touch the floor. That is a 1-D manifold in hip_pitch; the START member (idx 0) is its state with the largest front margin, settled 1.0 s under the canonical law holding its own joints (`measure_constants.settle`), accepted only if the env's `is_fallen_now` holds after settling, quiet (|qvel| < 0.05) and free of self-contact; idx 1..4 are its manifold neighbours (the start-condition family for the basin test). Definition and builder: `tools/morph02_seated.py`; families `seated` in every DEV constants file. Geometry conventions are GETUP-10B (CoM = `subtree_com[0]`; sole interval = sphere centres ± 4 mm; d_front = front sole edge − CoM; positive = CoM inside).

**The mechanism, decomposed (frozen foot).** With the pelvis on the floor,
`d_front = (front edge − ankle) + (ankle − hip) − (CoM − hip)`.
The first term is the frozen foot: **+54.0 mm on every plant**. The second is the fold: the shank must reach the floor from a knee that sits above a hip only ~58 mm off the floor, with the knee capped at −155° and the hip at 180°, so the ankle can never come closer than **≈ 94 mm behind the hip** (best of 36: U210_L200 −93.8; U130_L120 −99.5; canonical **−185.5**). The third is the mass: the legs are 73 % of the 4.221 kg (thigh 0.739 kg each, pelvis 1.128 kg), and in the fold they hang behind the hip, putting the CoM 20–47 mm behind the hip — not enough. The deficit is **10–15 mm on the ridge u ≈ l + 10 mm** (U130_L120 −10.1, U150_L140 −10.5, U210_L200 −13.3, U170_L160 −13.5) and **89.6 mm on the canonical geometry**; it grows fast off the ridge because a shank much longer than the thigh must angle back to reach the floor and a thigh much longer than the shank cannot fold. The ridge is a property of the fold, not of scale: shrinking both legs does not close it (U130_L120 is no better than U210_L200).

## 6. Phase D — post-fall reachability of the seated basin (separate from morphology selection)
