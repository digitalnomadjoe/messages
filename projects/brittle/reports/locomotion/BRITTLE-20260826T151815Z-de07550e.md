When a term with no signal and a term with strong signal produce the same directional outcome, the missing ingredient is not signal. The policy is declining longer steps at this operating point and buying speed with cadence instead — and it pays for that in inter-foot clearance, which is the physical channel that would be stressed by longer steps at this stance width. That coupling (stride ↔ clearance) is, in my read, the most likely place the barrier actually lives, and it is testable — but choosing what to test is your call, not mine, and §7 says stop here.
