## 2. Design

Construction = the C11S4F harness (plant-asserted, contract-checked) with three parameterized knobs (reset_roll, additive_dr, injection) + the per-arm `kd_mult`. Four arms per stratum, seeds {0,1}, 64 worlds, 400 steps (8 s), training-faithful terminations (`et_no_step 4.5`, `et_real`; `err_et` off as in every C11S4 eval):

| arm | ankle-roll kd | spawn |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 1.0 (card) | standing (lead-in frame 25) |
| A2 | 1.0 (card) | reference-injected, frame ~ U[0, 658) |
| A3 | 0.2 (diagnostic) | standing |
| A4 | 0.2 (diagnostic) | **the identical injected states as A2** |

**Run-1 instrument finding (kept honest):** the adapter's `arc_rsi` fires only on *respawns inside `step()`*, never at `reset()` — so a naive arc_rsi=1.0 experiment measures standing first episodes in every arm (run 1: A1≡A2 exactly). Run 2 therefore performs the injection itself at t=0 by replicating the adapter's own arc-RSI injection verbatim (`_bent_state` → `sim.set_state(..., reanchor=True)` → phase/tick/xnet/ynet/anchor → `prev_x/_prev_y_arc/prev_yaw/_he_seed` + `prev_foot_xy` refresh), followed by one zero-action settle step so the policy's first action sees the injected state through the env's own obs assembly. Identical procedure in every arm. This also corrects the C11S4F R5 claim that training RSI-injects *at t=0* — injection is respawn-time; the training exposure (~30% of respawns) stands.

**Pairing gate:** max |qpos_injected(A2) − qpos_injected(A4)| = **0.0 exactly**, both strata, both seeds — the Kd arms start from bit-identical reference states with identical frames (fixed-stream frame draws) and, in the DR stratum, identical per-world DR draws (same sim seed).

## 4. Decision — **CONFIRMED** (both strata, reported separately, agreeing)

Kd 1.0 selectively destabilizes reference spawns (0% standing → 83% reference under the same law) and Kd 0.2 materially rescues the identical states (83% → 8.6%, a 9.7× reduction on bit-identical initial conditions). The mechanism channels corroborate: under Kd 1.0 the ankle-roll velocity envelope reaches ~57 rad/s median (33× the standing baseline) with torque pinned at the 12.5 clip and median failure at 0.7 s — the discrete-time instability signature C11E established (armature 6.6e-5 × kd @500 Hz); under Kd 0.2 the same insertions show no oscillation growth (envelope ratio med 0.68–0.74 < 1, i.e. *decaying*).

**Bearing on D-14:** training spawned ~30% of respawns on reference frames under ankle-roll Kd 1.0; this falsifier measures ~83% of such spawns dying within ~1 s. That is a sufficient, measured mechanism for the trainer's fell-dominated young-death regime coexisting with a zero-fall standing-spawn eval. (Consistency, not exclusivity: stochastic-action and other contributions remain unmeasured — training uses sampled actions; this probe is deterministic.)

## 5. Secondary paired finding (out of scope to act on; recorded)

Ankle-roll Kd alone flips the **standing-spawn stall behavior**: at kd 1.0, 112/128 (NOM) / 56/128 (DR) of standing worlds stall on the 4.5 s no-real-step clock; at kd 0.2, **zero** worlds stall in either stratum (all censored at 8 s, still stepping) — i.e. under kd 0.2 the same frozen policy's touchdowns qualify as real steps. This ties D-13 (stance duration vs the step gate) to the ankle-roll damping and is directly relevant to any future reward/gating analysis — but the policy is out of its training law in the 0.2 arms, so interpretation stops at "the gate behavior is law-sensitive".

## 7. Caveats

- Deterministic `actor_mean`; training uses stochastic actions — absolute fall rates in training differ; the paired Kd contrast is the finding.
- The Kd 0.2 arms run the policy outside its training law; absolute performance there is not a capability claim.
- Injection frames drawn U[0, 658) including lead-in and the DS hold — frame difficulty is mixed but identical across arms (paired), and the A4 falls (11/128) plausibly concentrate in genuinely hard mid-swing states; per-frame breakdown is in the artifact npz-ready JSON.
- One zero-action settle step precedes the first policy action (declared; symmetric across arms; matches the "obs reflects injected state" semantics of the training respawn path).
- First-episode analysis only; post-respawn data ignored (respawn streams desync across arms).
- Same-seed same-config rebuilds reproduced run-1 arm statistics exactly in this configuration; cross-run MJWarp nondeterminism (C11S4E) was not re-measured.
- The stall clock runs from t=0 in all arms (kernel counters zeroed at reset; injection does not touch them) — identical censoring rules across arms.
