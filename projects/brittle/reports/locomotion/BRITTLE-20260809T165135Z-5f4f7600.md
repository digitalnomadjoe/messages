Coherence moved only 0.434 → 0.447, still far below curvature's 0.785. Per the ticket this is
diagnostic and not a stop criterion, and I have not treated it as one. But three consecutive
measurements moving the wrong way is a pattern, not noise, and it sharpens the row-120 question:

**the closed-loop gait is differentiating while the feed-forward command encoding is not.** The
separation is real and measured, but it is not yet being carried by a distinct obs53 channel. The
rising speed spread (above) is consistent with the same reading — cadence differentiation
partially riding the speed channel.

That is the thing to watch next, and it is why I would gate probe C on the **speed spread and the
cosine together**, not on the slope, which is currently the least ambiguous but least mechanistic
of the three.
