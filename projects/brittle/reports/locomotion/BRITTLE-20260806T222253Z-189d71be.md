## 0. Headline

**The live-command assertion failed, so the ticket's hard stop was honoured and no training was launched.** From the optimizer's own rollout buffer, with the corrected card:

| stratum | required | **measured** |
|---|---|---|
| straight | ≈ 33.3 % | **99.984 %** |
| left | ≈ 33.3 % | **0.004 %** |

| \|obs45\| > 0.05 | ≈ 66.7 % | **0.015 %** |

My correction moved the live fraction from Q3's 0.003 % to 0.015 % — real, and nowhere near the gate.

**★★★ The mechanism is structural, and it rules out the whole class of fix I attempted.** The turn command exists only inside a member's `yaw_cmd_profile` zone, which begins **~214 control steps after the member start**. `env.reset()` puts every world at its member start. A PPO rollout is **64 steps**. Arc-RSI fires **only on respawns inside `step()`, never at `reset()`**. Therefore **no correction that acts only at respawn time can satisfy an iteration-0 buffer proof** — the first rollout is command-free by construction, whatever the respawn policy does. Measured directly: live-command fraction is **0.00000 over the first 64 steps** and **0.66667 after 300 steps** (exactly the two turn members of three), in both the arc-RSI 0.85 and arc-RSI 0.0 configurations.

## 1. What was built, and what it did

It reuses the existing P17 member-scoped injector and its episode-start `set_state(reanchor=True)`, which is the correct place to zero `prev_action` (Q2's finding was that the same call is wrong for *mid-episode* injection of a continuing trajectory, not at a genuine respawn).

Card: Q3's card verbatim plus `--arc-rsi 1.0 --arc-rsi-zone --adv-norm-per-regime`, init unchanged at Q2 `b91ccf27ea5cbb88` (**not** a Q3 checkpoint), same plant, law v2, 54-D contract, action scale 0.20, members, oracle, equal member world counts, and the same anchor/trust configuration.

**Effect measured, not assumed:** at iteration 0 the exposure rose 0.003 % → 0.015 % — the handful of worlds that fell early and were injected into the zone by the new code. The edit works; it is simply the wrong lever for a cold first rollout.

**The trainer has been restored to its registered bytes.** Working tree sha is `a580c43724809482`, matching the registered `trainer-candidate-c15f` pin. ## 3. ★ Correction to my Q4 report

Q4's headline said *"the Q3 PPO run never presented the turn commands to the policy."* **That over-generalized.** The measurement behind it came from `--grad-audit-out`, which dumps and returns at **iteration 0** — a single cold rollout. It is solid evidence that Q3's **first** rollout was command-free; it is **not** evidence about the whole run.

Q3's `ep_len` rose **129.9 → 454.9** across its eight iterations, and **455 > 214**, so later Q3 iterations very likely did carry partial live commands. **I have not measured that fraction**, and I am not going to assert a number I did not measure: what is established is (a) iteration 0 was command-free, (b) exposure grows with episode length, and (c) the exposure across the Q3 run is *unknown and must be measured per iteration*, which needs `ADV_AUDIT_IT` at several iterations of a real run.

**What in Q4 still stands, unaffected:** the per-command gradient audit was run on *correctly-commanded* frozen batches, so left-vs-right cosine **−0.047**, exactly-zero entropy and anchor gradients, the healthy command-aware critic (32 % of value sd), the attenuating mixed step, and the **+0.336** mixed-normalization asymmetry are all independent of this error. Q4's decision `CAPACITY LIMIT NOT SUPPORTED` therefore still holds on those legs. Part A's repeatability results are likewise unaffected.

**What this does change:** Q4's *causal story* for Q3 — "PPO trained blind, so the anti-correlation was drift" — is weakened to "PPO trained with **degraded and time-varying** command exposure, starting from zero". That is still enough to make Q3's capacity reading unsupported, but it is a weaker claim than Q4 stated, and the honest position is that Q3's exposure profile is unmeasured.

1. **Seed the initial phase in-zone for curved members at reset.** The metadata is already on the adapter (`loop_lo`, `loop_span`, `_kappa_curved_w`), the cyclic loop then *keeps* worlds in the zone, and P27 keeps respawns there too. Straight members keep their existing reset distribution.
2. **Re-run this exact iteration-0 proof.** With in-zone seeding it can pass: ≈ 1/3 per command and ≈ 2/3 live, from the optimizer's own buffer.
3. **Only then** verify `--adv-norm-per-regime` is genuinely active (both turn strata above the 64-sample floor, near-zero means, comparable scale), and run preflight items 3–6.
4. **Also measure Q3's actual exposure profile** — `ADV_AUDIT_IT` at iterations 0, 3 and 7 of a replayed Q3 configuration — so the Q4 correction above is closed with numbers rather than an argument.

Items 1–2 are a single small edit plus a re-run of a proof that already exists and takes under a minute.
