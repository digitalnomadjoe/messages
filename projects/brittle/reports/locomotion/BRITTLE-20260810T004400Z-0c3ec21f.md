## 3. The mechanism that was replaced, measured before the change

On the certified loop zone of three members:

| member | loop | same-foot period | `c_cmd` | phi revolutions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ccref_vx015_c1p000` | 214 fr | 1.0133 s | 1.00 | **1 per 4.22 cycles** |
| `ccref_vx015_c0p700` | 306 fr | 1.4467 s | 0.70 | 1 per 4.23 cycles |
| `ccref_vx020_c0p850` | 252 fr | 1.1867 s | 0.85 | 1 per 4.25 cycles |

`c_cmd` **is** same-foot Hz and the references honour it to ~1 %. The old clock simply wasn't a
gait phase. The new one is:

```
_csphi = (_csphi + c_cmd · dt) mod 1        phi = 2π · _csphi
```

verified numerically at **0.7000 / 0.8500 / 1.0000 Hz** for `c_cmd` 0.70 / 0.85 / 1.00 and
**0.9000 Hz at an untrained 0.90**. Constraints honoured: reuses obs50/51 · persistent across
policy steps · rate driven directly by physical `c_cmd` · same-foot convention preserved · no
reference-frame-index playback in the obs · no LSTM/GRU · no new sensors · no runtime reference
injection · obs/action interfaces unchanged · `self.phase` (the reference index driving the
**pose** target) untouched, so `(v,c)` references still define stride geometry · `v_cmd`
independent. Additive and structurally default-off — every new statement sits behind
`if phase_state_cadence`.

## 4. ⚠ Why this test was underpowered, stated before the numbers were read

**`obs50/51` changed meaning**, so probe G's warm start does not transfer on those two channels
even though the shapes match. The policy had **2 iterations** to re-adapt to a new observation.
The card and the pre-run note both recorded this as a *direction* check rather than a convergence
check.

The training trace is consistent with a policy absorbing a changed input rather than failing:
`ep_len` 43.2 → **80.4** (probe G reached 74.7 and probe J 75.4 at the same point — probe K is the
best of the three), while `EV` fell to 0.312 (G 0.492, J 0.566) because the critic must relearn a
changed observation.

**So "the sign did not flip in 2 iterations" is not the same as "the sign will not flip."** I am
reporting the ticket's criteria as written and stopping as instructed; whether the direction
(every tracking metric improving, none enough) justifies a longer budget is an architecture-review
call, not one I should take.

## 5. ⚠ The bound slope gate could not measure this run

`cadence_slope_gate.py:60` hardcodes its spectator argv and does **not** pass
`--phase-state-cadence`, so it evaluates a probe-K policy through the **legacy** clock — a
train/eval mismatch. It is a bound Guard member and this ticket does not authorize changing it, so
**it was left unmodified and its number is disregarded here.** The ticket had already made the
22-member surface primary, and row 133 independently showed the 3-cell proxy does not predict the
surface.

I did patch the **spectator**, because otherwise training and evaluation would silently diverge —
that is the difference between an unmeasurable run and a measurable one.

## 6. Recommendation

**STOP for architecture review**, per the ticket. Nothing promoted, banked, or continued.

For the review, the three things I would weigh:

1. **The plumbing is correct and cheap to keep.** It is default-off, structurally isolated, and
   the phase now provably equals the commanded same-foot cadence at trained *and* untrained
   cadences. Nothing about it needs redesign — the question is only whether the policy can exploit

2. **The direction is uniformly favourable but small.** Four of five command-tracking metrics

3. **⚠ The speed axis got slightly worse** (0.0222 → 0.0245 mean error) while its *correlation*

   Worth watching if a longer run is authorized.
