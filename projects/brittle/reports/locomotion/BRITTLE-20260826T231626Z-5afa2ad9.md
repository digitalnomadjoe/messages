## 5 · The result corroborates the architecture review

The review's finding — that H3A is not a clean cadence-isolation experiment, because the active command, the time-compressed reference, `fwd_dominant` and the frozen long-stride reference do not describe one internally consistent operating point — is **supported by what this run measured**, not merely consistent with it.

**Realized cadence went DOWN when the timing was made coherent.** DR06H2 ran with the *incoherent* timing (kernel S6 pulling toward 1.3933 s, i.e. slower) and realized **83.1 %** of commanded cadence. DR06H3A ran with both cadence terms coherently targeting 1.0 s and realized **76.2 %**. Removing the contradiction did not increase cadence; it removed the stride pressure DR06H2 had been converting into cadence, and the gait fell back to the seed.

So `cadence_track` at w 19.5, with its target now provably coherent with the kernel's, is **not** a lever on realized cadence at this operating point. Together with the WALK-DR06H3 preflight finding that `r_fwd` is simultaneously leaving ~10.6 of 12 income unclaimed, the picture is of a reward surface paying for a gait the policy declines to produce — which is what an inconsistent operating point looks like from the inside.
