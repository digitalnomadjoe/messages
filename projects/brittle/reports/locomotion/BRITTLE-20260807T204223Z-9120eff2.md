## 1. The synthesis rule — one line, and it reuses the committed mechanism verbatim

```
member(v, c)  =  stride_scale( resample(base, r), r )        r = c_base / c
```

with the speed axis composed on top as the existing `stride_scale(v/v₀)` at fixed cadence.

**Why this is exactly right, and why it is not a retime:**

- `resample(base, r)` stretches the cycle in **time** — that is the cadence — and as a side effect divides the per-frame forward displacement by `r`, which alone would change the speed.
- `stride_scale(r)` multiplies the forward advance back by `r` (**speed restored to `v`**) *and* scales the **spatial step excursion** — hip-pitch deviation, mean-preserving, plus the foot-relative fore-aft channel — by the same `r`. **That is the geometry change.**
- Net: speed fixed at `v`, cycle period `1/c`, spatial advance per cycle `= v/c = stride`, and the step excursion scales with the stride. **Timing and geometry both move.**

★ **Nominal reproduction is structural, not tuned.** At `c = c_base`, `r = 1`: `resample` is the identity and `stride_scale(1)` is the identity. Measured: **max |Δ| = 0.000e+00** on `tpos`, `trpy`, `jq`, `rf_rel`, `lf_rel`, and both contact schedules **bit-identical**.

`stride_scale` is **imported** from the committed `rl/scripts/build_crown_command_family.py`, not copied. It touches `tpos[:,0]`, `jq[:,2]`, `jq[:,8]` and `{rf,lf}_rel[:,0]` — **nothing else** — so knee, ankle, hip-yaw and hip-roll are untouched, as required.

| **this** | 1.000 | 0.1530 | 0.2224 | 1.454 |

**Both families reduce step excursion as cadence rises** — ccref4b 0.769 → 0.410 (ratio 0.532), this mechanism 0.262 → 0.186 (ratio 0.707). The trend is physically coherent in the same direction, which is what the ticket asked for rather than a bare numeric threshold.

⚠ **A real limitation, stated plainly.** This mechanism is **first-order linear**: swing/stride is constant by construction (1.454–1.458). `ccref4b`, sourced from a real realized gait, has a *non-constant* ratio (2.334 / 2.542 / 1.624). So these references are geometrically self-consistent but do **not** reproduce the non-linear way a real gait redistributes excursion with cadence. A second limitation of the same kind: `stride_scale` puts the whole excursion change on **hip-pitch**, leaving knee and ankle untouched, whereas a real gait would move all three. Both are properties of the committed mechanism that Route 1 deliberately reuses; the DFL gate at family-generation time is where they should be caught if they matter.
