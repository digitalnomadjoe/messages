## 3. The mechanism — the latent went silent, and I can show it three ways

**(a) Gradient norms, straight from the run log, both iterations:**

```
it=1  GN=tl:0.004 | p0:0.000 | p2:0.797 | an:4.328
it=2  GN=tl:0.005 | p0:0.000 | p2:0.735 | an:4.242
```

**`tlat` receives 0.004 against the action head's 4.328 — roughly one part in a thousand.** Once
the trunk and head are free to move, the optimiser routes essentially all of the gradient through
them and none through the latent. `p0:0.000` independently confirms `policy_net.0` stayed frozen.

**(b) The latent's learned authority collapsed versus probe I** (same producer, same 8 × 3 grid):

| channel | probe I (trunk frozen) | **probe J (trunk plastic)** | cadence-axis range I → J |
|---|---|---|---|
| gain_sin | 15.23 % of bound | **2.49 %** | 0.005049 → **0.000809** |
| gain_cos | 12.26 % | **1.98 %** | 0.004088 → **0.000719** |
| bias_sin | 11.65 % | **0.27 %** | 0.003904 → **0.000087** |
| bias_cos | 3.25 % | **0.56 %** | 0.000956 → **0.000212** |

A **6× collapse** on the leading channel and up to **45×** on `bias_sin`.

**(c) The producer's own verdict flipped.** Probe I read *"command-conditioned modulation
present."* Probe J reads **"COMMAND-INDEPENDENT — the latent exerts effectively no
command-conditioned modulation."** The cadence-axis ranges fell below the 1e-3 threshold.

**This directly confirms the trainer's inline comment at `:3769`** — *"U1 showed the latent goes
silent once the trunk trains."* In the pre-registration I flagged that the U1 *report* did not
establish this, because its documented runs carried no latent at all. **Probe J establishes it,
with its own gradient telemetry and its own latent measurement.** The comment was right; the
citation behind it was simply not the U1 report.

## 7. Recommendation — retire the stateless timing-latent approach

Per the ticket: **NEGATIVE ⇒ STOP.** No long PPO is prepared. No further weight or FiLM-bound
probes, as instructed — and §3 is why that instruction is correct: raising the bounds cannot help
a module receiving one part in a thousand of the gradient. The bound was never the binding
constraint.

**What the four probes now establish together:**

| | trunk frozen | trunk plastic |
|---|---|---|
| **λ = 0.95** | F: −2.3562, latent command-conditioned | — |
| **λ = 0.99** | I: −2.1017, latent command-conditioned, no behaviour | **J: −2.0823, latent SILENT, worse than doing nothing** |

- The latent **can** learn command-conditioned structure — but only while the trunk is frozen,
  where it cannot affect behaviour (row 131).
- The moment the trunk can move, the optimiser abandons the latent (§3) and the latent's presence
  is a net cost (§2).
- **There is no configuration of this pathway in which it both learns command structure and
  affects behaviour.** That is the retirement case, and it is now evidence rather than inference.

**Returning for the recurrent/phase-state architecture decision.** The standing Stage-B lesson —
differentiation must live in recurrent/phase **state**, not in a stateless map — is consistent
with every result here: a stateless FiLM has no way to hold a command-dependent *timing* that
persists across the gait cycle, so the only stable optimum available to it is a constant.

**Also still open and untouched by this probe:** the row-129 speed band. The control sits at
0.0195 and probe J at 0.0234, both from a probe-G baseline of 0.0164 — the band degrades under
continued training with or without the latent.
