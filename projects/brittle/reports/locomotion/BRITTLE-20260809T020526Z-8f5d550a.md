## 3. Actor sensitivity — the command is heard, and heard *identically* to speed

| CY | 0.3296 | 0.3347 | **1.015** |
| change | −0.0090 (−2.7 %) | −0.0105 (−3.0 %) | — |

Two facts:

1. **obs53 is not inert** — the actor responds to it with mean gain 0.33. This is not a wire that
   fails to reach the policy.
2. **The actor's gain on the cadence command equals its gain on the speed command to within
   2 %.** That is the mechanistic signature of the qualification's "not two axes" finding: to
   this actor the two command channels are near-interchangeable.

The joints that respond most to `obs53` are **R_ank_roll 0.75, L_hip_yaw 0.51, L_ank_roll 0.55**
— lateral and steering joints — while the sagittal gait joints a cadence command should drive
(`hip_pitch` 0.22, `knee` 0.37) respond less. The cadence channel is being read by the wrong
limb subspace.

Acquisition changed every one of these numbers by ~3 %.

## 4. Training signal — the cadence term paid **exactly zero**

From the run's **own** receipts, not from reading code:

```
train.log : [reward-contract] ss_tempo_w=0.0  stance_cap_w=0.35  fall_cost=50.0
ckpt_meta : "p_tempo": 0.00000
trainer   : p_under  = -ss_tempo_w * ended * max(ss_target_frames - ss_dur, 0)   (:2500)
            p_over   = -ss_tempo_w * max(ss_dur - ss_target_frames, 0)           (:2503)
            p_tempo  = p_under + p_over                                          (:2504)
```

With `ss_tempo_w = 0.0`, `p_tempo ≡ 0` identically. **The only reward term that reads the
per-world cadence target contributed exactly nothing for the entire run** — while the target
itself was computed correctly (1.429 / 1.176 / 1.000 s) and printed in the log.

Answering the section's questions precisely:

* **cadence tracking target seen by the reward:** correct, per-world, rate-normalized — and
  multiplied by zero.
* **reward contribution by cadence stratum:** `p_tempo = 0.000` for **every** stratum. There is
  no per-stratum difference to report because there is no contribution.
* **advantage mean/sign by stratum:** not separable from this run's frozen receipts — the
  per-stratum advantage buffer is not persisted, and I will not infer it. Stated as a gap.
* **were low-cadence worlds rewarded for high realized cadence?** **No.** They were neither
  rewarded nor penalized for realized cadence. This is not a mislabelled signal — it is an
  **absent** one.

⚠ Honest qualifier: the imitation terms (`track_feet` +2.24, `track_jnt` +3.24) *do* carry
cadence information implicitly, because each world tracks its own correctly-paced reference
through the phase clock. So the cadence gradient was not literally zero — but the one **direct,
explicit** cadence term was, and 3 PPO iterations is far too little for an implicit
phase-mediated signal to reshape a command axis.

## 5. Root classification

# `INVERTED SEED NOT OVERCOME`

**Confidence: HIGH.** Supported by four independent measurements: the seed carries the full
inversion on an identical sweep (−2.800 vs −2.703); the command semantics are monotone and
correct at all nine traced stages; actor sensitivity to `obs53` moved 2.7 % during acquisition;
and the run's own ledger shows the direct cadence term paid `0.00000`.

The two candidate labels are not in competition, and it is worth being exact about how they
relate: **the seed supplied the inversion, and `ss_tempo_w = 0.0` guaranteed it would survive.**
Classifying this as a *semantics* bug would be wrong — the chain is clean. Classifying it as a
pure *training-signal* bug would misplace the origin — the wrong sign predates this unit
entirely.

And the budget makes the outcome overdetermined: **442,368 env steps is 3 PPO iterations, 0.3
minutes of wall time.** No command axis is acquired in three iterations, with or without a
reward term.

re-run one bounded acquisition.**

Why this and nothing else:

* it is **one existing flag** on an **already-wired** mechanism — `p_tempo`, its per-world
  target, and its plumbing all exist and are already correct;
* it requires **no reward redesign, no new term, no reference change, no gait change**, all of

**Two conditions before that run is worth paying for**, both of which follow from this RCA:

   correctly-*ordered* amount across the three cadence strata **before** spending a full
   acquisition on it. The term penalizes single-support **dwell** against a cadence-derived
   target — a cadence-adjacent proxy, not same-foot cadence measured directly — so its ordering
   must be measured, not assumed.
