## 1. `path_speed_v1` multi-segment aggregation — **Red oracle 0 FAIL of 26**

The weight is now the **raw traversed time**, `weight_i = (n_i − 1)·dt`, exactly as specified.

**Why the previous weight was wrong, stated as a mechanism rather than a patch:** the one-cycle box filter trims `L_i − 1` frames from *every* segment, and that trim is a larger **fraction** of a short segment. Weighting by the measured (smoothed) duration `m_i·dt` therefore under-weights short segments — so a short *fast* segment beside a long *slow* one is pulled down. That is precisely `C8b_short_fast_long_slow`, and it is why the error was one-sided (−8.19 %, never +).
