## 7 · Mechanism findings

1. **The encoder-noise cliff is the ESTIMATOR, not the policy.** Split probe (σ=0.5°, crown + κ−1.0 cells): noise into the policy obs only → survives 700/700; noise into the leg-odometry estimator only → falls @44/@39. The deployed estimator differentiates sole FK at 50 Hz with α=1.0 (no smoothing), so σ_q≈0.5° ⇒ ~0.13 m/s of velocity noise on obs 6:9 — 65% of walking speed. Consequences: (a) obs-noise DR training must include the estimator in the loop or it trains against the wrong plant; (b) a zero-training estimator filter (α<1 or proper differencing) may recover most of the margin — but the estimator is part of the frozen deployed FUNCTION, so touching it is Joe's interface call. The collapse reproduces 12/12 on BOTH engines at σ=0.5° (§6) — engine-independent, exactly as an estimator-side mechanism predicts.
2. **Gain accuracy ≫ torque headroom.** A hard ceiling at 5 N·m (×0.4) produces zero falls and quality-only drift, while a proportional gain error ×0.7 kills. Nominal walking τ99 is 3.5–4.2 N·m against the 12.5 clip. Actuator selection for THIS gait is not torque-bound; drive calibration is. (Does not license downsizing: push-recovery/F8 loads were not in this battery.)
3. **DR shape-specificity is real and measured.** The Crown trained under additive_dr's binary 0/20 ms whole-step action delay: at exactly that kind it is near-clean (1/12), while never-seen 14–18 ms substep delays fall 1–5/12. DR buys the distribution you sample, not the neighborhood around it — the same lesson as the kp arc (DR01 §0.6), now on a second axis.
4. **The lateral axis is the terrain weakness** — 3–4° cross-slope vs −10° uphill. The SPEED+CURVATURE command interface has no lateral-offset channel; drift accumulates unopposed (1.4 m per 13 s at 3°). Any sandbox progression hits this wall first.
5. **kd×2 reproduces the banked ankle-roll discrete-instability law** (armature 6.6e-5 × kd 0.4 @500 Hz → fall @22 steps with 22.5° tilt) — the DR04 map independently re-derives the kd≤0.2 ankle-roll repair from the outside.
