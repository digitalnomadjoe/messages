## 2. ⚠ I am not calling symmetry a regression — there is no noise floor for it

Stance-fraction asymmetry read 0.0273 → 0.0384. Swing-clearance asymmetry was flat
(0.0527 → 0.0524). In absolute terms the stance fractions are ~0.53 / 0.55.

**But symmetry has never had its noise floor measured**, and this session has twice shown that
gating on an uncharacterised quantity is how you get a wrong answer: row 135's speed *span* turned
out to have ±5 points of noise and carried no information, and row 137's speed-error threshold sat
exactly *on* its noise and would have ended the referendum a checkpoint before the peak.

A single-seed 0.0273 → 0.0384 move on a quantity with no floor **cannot establish a regression**.
I am reporting it as **unmeasurable**, not as a failure, and it needs a floor (3 seeds, same
checkpoint) before it can gate anything.

## 3. Why this is saturation, not a plateau to push through

| P1 | 4.55 % → 12 % (+7.45 pts) | 0.0978 → 0.1192 (+0.0214 m) | **2.87 mm / pt** |
| **P2** | 12 % → 16 % (+4.00 pts) | 0.1192 → 0.1230 (+0.0038 m) | **0.95 mm / pt** |

**3× less stride per share point**, and stride/target essentially static at 0.65× → 0.66×. The
allocation lever is spent: the references at vx=0.153 already ask for 0.1974–0.2003 m and the
policy delivers 0.123. Adding more worlds on the same references does not change what the policy
chooses to do on them.

That is the diagnosis the 0.65× ratio implied in row 139, now confirmed by a second data point:
**the remaining gap is not a command-allocation problem.**

## 4. Recommendation

**STOP increasing the share**, per your stop condition. Do not run P3 on this lever.

**The decision you now hold: is a stride-directed reward worth adding?** The evidence for it is
that two allocation steps moved the target from 0.1839 → 0.2003 while realized stride went
0.0978 → 0.1230 — the policy tracks about two-thirds of what it is asked and the fraction is not
improving. Nothing short of changing what is *rewarded* looks likely to close that.

The evidence for caution: P2 improved **speed error, slope, correlation, pelvis nod and clearance
simultaneously**. The gait is in its best measured state. A new reward term risks that, and the
arc has repeatedly shown reward changes trading one axis for another (rows 130, 137).

**If you authorize it**, I would want a symmetry noise floor measured first, so the new term can be
gated on all nine criteria rather than eight.
