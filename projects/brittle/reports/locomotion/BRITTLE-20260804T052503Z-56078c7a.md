## 2. Diagnosis (Joe's taxonomy)

- **PRIMARY — excessive acceleration immediately after lift-off:** the review policy completes **40% of forward travel in the first 25% of swing** (ref 24%, min-jerk 16%) — a lunge, then a coast.
- **PRIMARY — vertical/horizontal timing mismatch with a flat skim:** apex at **half the reference's clearance** (42 vs 81 mm) and late (u 0.62 vs 0.50) — the foot rushes forward low, skims, and then drops.
- **PRIMARY — unbraked vertical touchdown:** −0.455 m/s vs the reference's −0.188 (2.4×) — the slap that dominates the visual impression. Horizontal arrival is *slower* than ref (0.114 vs 0.177) — braking happens in the wrong axis.
- NOT the source: late-swing catch-up (opposite pattern), action/setpoint jerk (2.65/s modest; policy jerk 50 < ref 106), phase lag (0.00 — the swing is phase-locked), stance-entry slip (2.4 mm, clean).
- **Reference verdict:** the c344 profile is only quasi-eased — its lift-off easing is absent (fwd25 ≈ constant-speed) and its 50 Hz jerk RMS (106) exceeds the policy's; this imperfection is real and reported, but the policy's defects are 1.7–2.4× beyond it in the three primary axes. **The policy deviates geometrically AND temporally from the reference; the reference is not the cause of the visual defect.**

## 3. Why the policy does this (reward economics, measured)

1. `r_clearance` saturates at `rp_h_star` = 30 mm — at 42 mm apex the channel is already capped; **nothing pays climbing toward the reference's 80 mm**, while action/torque costs pay staying low → the flat skim is economically rational.
2. **Touchdown velocity is entirely unpriced** (no impact term; stomp tax measured ≈ −0.005/step) → the slap is free.
3. `track_feet` at σ_foot = 60 mm keeps **76% of its income at the measured 45 mm swing error** — the phase-locked position channel (which *is* velocity-timing-sensitive by construction: wrong intra-swing timing = wrong position at phase) is simply too loose to bind. At σ 35 mm the same error keeps 44% (lost income 2.2/step of 4 — a live, learnable gradient); at 25 mm, 20%.

## 4. Reward audit answer

Position-at-phase tracking IS structurally sufficient to price velocity timing, lift-off lunge, skim, and the pre-touchdown state — **at a discriminating σ**. A velocity/acceleration-aware term is NOT necessary as the first step; it becomes the fallback only if σ-tightening under-delivers (second fallback: `rp_h_star` 0.03 → 0.06 to restore a clearance gradient toward the reference's apex; third: an event-scoped, alternation-latched touchdown-velocity penalty).

## 5. Recommendation (exactly one)

**Tighten `σ_foot` 60 → 35 mm** in the landed additive-imitation config — one existing constant, at the measured error scale (C11S9 discipline), pricing all three primary defects through the already-proven channel. Not landed (read-only unit).
