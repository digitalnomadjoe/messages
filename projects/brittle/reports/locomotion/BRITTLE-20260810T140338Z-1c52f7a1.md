## 3. Why this is a stop, not a weight problem

The weight was deliberately conservative: same event structure as `cadence_track_w`, at **8.0 =
41 %** of its 19.5, per your "minimally calibrated, subordinate" requirement and no-sweep rule.

A larger weight is the obvious next thought, and I am **not** recommending it. At 8.0 the term
already produced measurable costs on **two** quality axes (nod +5.6 %, touchdown +7.6 %) for
**+1.1 %** of stride. That exchange rate gets worse, not better, with weight — and this arc has
repeatedly shown reward increases trading axes rather than adding (rows 130, 137).

Combined with P2's allocation saturation (2.87 → 0.95 mm per share point), **both available levers
now return ~1 % stride for measurable quality cost.** The policy tracks ~68 % of the scheduled
stride and does not want to go further under this objective set.
