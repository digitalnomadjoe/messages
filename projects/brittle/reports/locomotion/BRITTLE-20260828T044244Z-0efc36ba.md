## 4 · Three of my own evidence lines, retracted

1. **`max|a_req| = 0.8183` is a builder constant, not a property of any candidate.** Measured with pinned paths across the DR06C family: 0.818299 with 0.000 % outside for vx026, vx028, vx030, vx032 **and** vx035 — identical for gate-PASSING and gate-FAILING members alike. It has **zero discriminating power** and supports no feasibility distinction. It is also a *lower bound*: the deployed law is a 10 Hz ACD lag feeding `tau = clip(kp(acd_c − q) − kd·qd, ±12.5)`, so the command must lead the reference; adding only the kinematic velocity lead takes max `|a|` to ≈ 0.875, and `tau_ref/kp = 3.93/20 = 0.197 rad` is ≈ 0.98 action units on the 0.2-scale hip-yaw joints. "0.000 % outside" is the zero-error, zero-torque idealisation.

2. **The preservation-geometry argument is a category error and also constant.** `n = 648`, DS fraction 0.1929, mean |Δy| 76.77 mm and min |Δy| 51.29 mm are **bit-identical across the whole c = 1.0 family including vx035** — so "direct sibling of the preserved 0.24 member" licenses the gate-failing 0.35 exactly as strongly. Separately, preservation in this arc is measured **on a policy by rollout** (DR06H1 §5: clearance, torque, F8, DS bookends); reference-geometry similarity licenses nothing about behaviour retention when the training distribution changes, and DR06H1 is the in-arc counter-example. Two further defects in that artifact: "stance width 76.8 mm" is the mean |Δy| over **all** frames including swing crossing (DS-only mean is 99.25 mm), and `max_foot_z_mm` came out **NaN** — I presented a preservation comparison with the clearance channel unmeasured.

3. **A provenance bug in my own script.** `h3b_contract.py` resolved references with `glob.glob(...)[0]`. `ccref_vx032_c1p000.npz` has **two distinct contents**; `glob[0]` loaded the July 604-frame file, not the DR06C 648-frame one — and that loaded file has max `|a_req| = 1.0336` with **0.662 % of frames outside the command box**, an action-infeasible member sitting undetected in my comparison table. `ccref_vx024_c1p000.npz` has 5 copies / 2 distinct contents, likewise mis-resolved. This is the same defect class I disclosed in DR06H1. **References must be pinned by full path plus hash**, and the contract in §3 does so.

## 5 · Preservation risks

- **Lateral margin is the binding channel and 0.28 sits 0.8 mm inside the bar.** The progression −15.5 → −16.7 → −19.3 mm is monotone in speed; 0.28 is the last rung inside it. Any drift in stance width, sole geometry or plant would push it out.
- **Preservation is unproven, not proven.** No policy, checkpoint or rollout exists at 0.28. DS entry / behaviour / DS exit and foot-foot collision can only be established by the existing deterministic rollout harness **after** a policy exists; nothing in this preflight substitutes for it.
- **The DS-fraction statistic rests on a known-defective channel.** RED DR06R3A established that this family's contact booleans are authored independently of the geometry and are wrong at handoff (negative sole clearance at frames the schedule calls airborne). Any DS-based reasoning here inherits that defect.
- **The unscaled knee/ankle construction is inherited.** The recommended 0.280 member is built by the same `stride_scale` that opens only the hip. If the FK/foot-channel divergence is the mechanism behind the lateral wall, 0.280 sits on the same defective construction with less of it, not free of it.
- **Provenance/plant currency.** The DR06C family (n = 648) post-dates canonical plant v2; the July `ccref4b` lineage (n = 604) does not. Mixing them — which my script did — compares across a plant change.
