## 2. ⚠ The trunk was frozen — this is a clean isolation, and probe F was the same

The run's own log:

```
[reftrack] FREEZE (stage=0): 7 frozen, 10 trainable: ['mlp_extractor.value_net', 'tlat', 'value_net']
```

`--timing-latent` sets `module_only` (`train_tip_reftrack.py:3767`), so the trainable set is
**`tlat` + the critic only**. The actor trunk cannot move.

Two consequences, and they cut in opposite directions:

- **Good:** probe I is a *perfect* isolation. Any behavioural change is 100 % attributable to the
  latent. That is why two cells came back **bit-identical** to probe G (c=0.70 and c=0.85 cadence
  agree to all printed digits) — the trunk is literally the same network and the cadence estimator
  is quantised in touchdown counts.
- **⚠ Load-bearing:** the latent is being asked to fix command blindness **through a frozen policy
  that is command-blind.** It can only re-index responses the trunk already has; it cannot create
  a cadence sensitivity the trunk lacks. Rows 125/126 established that the trunk has no distinct
  cadence direction. §5 argues this is the actual ceiling.

## 5. ⚠ Correcting the record: probe F's latent was NOT a command-independent constant

The ticket's rationale — and my own launcher comment and pre-registration, both written before
this measurement — repeated row 126's claim that probe F's latent *"learned a command-independent
constant."* Because that claim is the premise of this whole probe, I measured probe F's surviving
checkpoint with the same producer instead of trusting the prior wording. **It does not hold.**

| channel | **F** cadence-axis range | **I** cadence-axis range | **F** % of bound | **I** % of bound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gain_sin | 0.004757 | 0.005049 | 15.28 % | 15.23 % |
| gain_cos | **0.008785** | 0.004088 | **29.91 %** | 12.26 % |
| bias_sin | **0.005414** | 0.003904 | 17.65 % | 11.65 % |
| bias_cos | **0.005457** | 0.000956 | 18.19 % | 3.25 % |

**Probe F's latent was command-conditioned, monotone, cadence-dominant (0.008785 across cadence
vs 0.003555 across vx), and STRONGER than probe I's on three of four channels.** Row 126's
characterisation was wrong, and this report supersedes it on that point.

So the intended experiment — *"F failed because the latent couldn't differentiate; the repaired
credit path should let it"* — rests on a premise that was not true. The latent already
differentiated at λ = 0.95. Giving it a longer credit horizon produced a **weaker** latent with
the same qualitative shape, and the same null effect on behaviour.

## 7. Recommendation — STOP for architecture decision

Per the ticket: **NEGATIVE ⇒ stop.** No long PPO is prepared and none should be. What follows is
input for your decision, not a knob I am turning.

**The most consistent reading of §2 + §4 + §5:** the pathway is not credit-starved and not
representation-starved. It learns the right shape at both horizons. It fails because a bounded
FiLM on two phase channels, applied to a **frozen** trunk, can only re-index responses the trunk
already has — and rows 125/126 established the trunk has no distinct cadence direction to
re-index. **The latent is being asked to cure command blindness through a command-blind policy.**
That is an inference, not a measurement, but it is the only reading consistent with: correct
structure, adequate command-conditioning, and zero behavioural effect at two horizons.

**What that implies for the three obvious options**, without recommending among them:

1. **Unfreeze with the latent** (`--unfreeze-stage 1` or `2`) — lets the trunk co-adapt so the
   latent has something to steer. ⚠ The trainer's own comment at `:3769` warns *"U1 showed the
   latent goes silent once the trunk trains,"* so this has already been tried and has a known
   failure mode. I would want that U1 evidence re-read before spending on it.
2. **Raise the FiLM bounds** past 0.2 — cheapest test of the authority hypothesis, but §4 shows
   only ~20 % of the learned magnitude is command-conditioned, so a larger bound may simply grow
   the constant.
3. **Differentiation in recurrent/phase state** rather than a stateless FiLM — this is what the
   standing Stage-B lesson points at, and it is a genuine architecture change, not a probe.

**My honest position:** options 1 and 2 are cheap enough to be worth one bounded probe each, but
§5 means I can no longer claim the latent pathway is "untested at long credit" — it is tested, at
both horizons, and it does not reach behaviour either way. The speed band, meanwhile, remains the
open blocker from row 129 and nothing in this probe touched it (0.0167, in band, unchanged).
