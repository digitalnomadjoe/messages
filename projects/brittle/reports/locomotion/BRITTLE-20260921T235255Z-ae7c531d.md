## 9. Camera control (owner addendum)

**Diagnosis (exact override):** `rl/demo01/demo01_isaac_live.py` called `sim.set_camera_view(eye, target)` on every rendered policy step (50 Hz; every 2 steps in the first version) with the target locked to the pelvis — one line, nothing else (no replay code, no prim attachment, no viewport reset). Any operator orbit/pan/zoom was overwritten within 20 ms.

**Fix:** `--cam free` is the DEFAULT: exactly ONE `set_camera_view` at spawn (initial framing), then zero writes — the operator owns the viewport. `--cam follow` / [X] button (or `cam` field in a scripted timeline) toggles FOLLOW: a world-referenced target smoothed with τ 0.6 s, eye = target + the operator's offset captured at the moment of the switch (so an orbit/zoom chosen in FREE is kept), updated per rendered step; switching back to FREE releases ownership immediately (no snap-back).
