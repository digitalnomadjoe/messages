## 5. ★★★ Trainer dry proof — three blockers, none worked around

Per the amendment I made **no trainer change**. Every check below calls or reads committed code.

### BLOCKER B2 — the resolver cannot express the sparse lattice

`resolve_ref_family_multi` builds `combos = [(v, c) for v in vset for c in cset]` — a **Cartesian product**. The 19-member lattice uses 9 `(v,c)` cells drawn from `vset = {0.10, 0.13, 0.153, 0.184, 0.24}` × `cset = {0.85, 1.00, 1.20}` = **15 cells**. Six cells are absent **by design**: (0.10,1.00), (0.13,1.00), (0.13,1.20), (0.184,0.85), (0.184,1.00), (0.24,1.00).

Executed against the built family, the resolver fails closed exactly as it should:

```
BLOCKER manifold: missing member for (vx=0.1, c=1.0): .../refs/ccref_vx010_c1p000.npz
```

It can produce 15 straight members, or up to 45 with curved variants — **never 19**. Filling the rectangle would mean training on 6 cells the lattice deliberately excludes, which is the thing the amendment forbids. **Sparse support is a resolver change; not made, returned.**

### BLOCKER B3 — the 9-stratum advantage normalisation does not exist

`--adv-norm-per-regime` implements exactly **four hardcoded masks**:

```python
_rmask = ((th.abs(_k45) <= 0.0062) & ~_hold, (_k45 > 0.0062) & ~_hold,
          (_k45 < -0.0062) & ~_hold, _hold)          # _hold = mb_obs[:, 53] < 0.05
```

with `_regime_telem = [... for _ in range(4)]`. Findings:

- **there is no cadence dimension in the partition at all** — the only use of obs53 is a binary stand/hold test, so κ-sign × cadence-band (9 strata) is unimplemented;
- the strata key on **obs45 = v·κ**, which is speed-dependent — the same κ falls on different sides of the 0.0062 threshold at different speeds;
- the min-count is 64, and **the sub-64 fallback to global statistics is silent**: the block simply does not overwrite `_advn` for that mask, with no log line and no abort. "Cannot silently fall back" is not satisfiable with the current code regardless of sample counts.

The SR design's arithmetic (smallest stratum 13,568 samples/iteration) is correct **for a partition that does not exist**. **Implementing it is a trainer change; not made, returned.**

### BLOCKER B4 — obs45 is one step stale after reset

- **obs52 / obs53 — fresh, conditionally.** Both are read live at obs-build time (`self.vx_cmd`, `self.cadence_cmd / 2.0`). With **`--cmd-retime-range 0`** they are per-world member constants that never change on reset, so they are always fresh. With retime > 0 they are **resampled after the observation has already been built** (resample block at char 206367, obs build at char 204332) and the post-reset obs52/53 would be stale. **This is why `--cmd-retime-range 0` must be explicit**, and it is the setting I verified against.
- **obs45 — stale by one step.** In the `cmdphase54` contract `_augment_obs` passes `full_obs[:, :50]` through unchanged; the `out[:, 45] = ref_wb[ph]` overwrite exists only in `_augment_obs_v3/v4`, which this contract does not use. So obs45 comes from the GPU sim's obs buffer. `sim.set_yaw_cmd_per_world(...)` **is** called with the post-respawn phase (verified: the call precedes the obs build in source order), but it only takes effect on the *next* sim obs launch — the observation returned by this step was computed before it. **A world that just respawned acts once on the pre-respawn yaw command.**

  This is benign when κ is constant across the loop *and* the respawn lands inside the loop, because then profile[pre] == profile[post] — true for these members in the loop interior. It is **not** benign for a respawn onto a hold/standing frame, where the profile is ~0 while the pre-reset value was v·κ. **A fix is a trainer change; not made, returned.**

The feasible region is **not a rectangle**, so I am reporting it as a speed-dependent curvature limit rather than forcing a box:

- **(C)** treat the DFL bars as the thing to revisit — the gate self-labels `[PROVISIONAL dynamic-gait regime]` and its −17.5 mm / 2.7 mm·s constants come from a 20-trajectory calibration corpus. **I am not proposing this**; changing a blocking gate to admit references it currently rejects is a C6 matter and would need its own unit and Joe's authorization.
