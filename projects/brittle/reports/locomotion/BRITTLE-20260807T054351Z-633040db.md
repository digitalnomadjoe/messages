## 1. What the command actually is (traced, not assumed)

The adapter drives the command channel through
`sim.set_yaw_cmd_per_world(_ycmd_prof[phase] * _retime_w)` → **obs45**, so the signed-curvature command *is* the yaw-rate profile and `kappa_cmd = profile / vx`. The trained endpoints are ±0.306 rad/s at vx 0.153, i.e. **`kappa_cmd = ±2.0 rad/m`**. A normalized fraction **F** therefore means `profile = 0.306·F`, `kappa_cmd = 2.0·F`.

**Substrate.** The sweep injects the kP200 profile, scaled by F, into the **straight member's** command slice. That makes the substrate **sign-neutral**, so `+F` and `−F` are exact mirrors of each other by construction and any measured left/right difference is the robot, not the reference. `F = 0` reduces to the canonical straight cell exactly.

★ **The substrate is provably inert.** Control cells re-ran the canonical kP200 and kM200 members on their own references: deterministic realized curvature **+1.1050 vs +1.1050 and −1.0880 vs −1.0880 — delta 0.0000 on both sides.** The reference geometry has no effect on realized behaviour, exactly as Part A0 predicted (it never enters the control law). Stochastic deltas 0.029 / 0.014 are evaluation noise.

## 6. The two blemishes, stated plainly

**(a) The response saturates in the top 5 % of command.** The only two non-monotone pairs on the 23-point grid are `(−1.00, −0.95)` and `(+0.95, +1.00)`, in *both* modes. Deterministic: \|κ\| is 1.1062 at F = −0.95 vs 1.0880 at F = −1.00, and 1.1102 at +0.95 vs 1.1050 at +1.00 — the curve is **flat-to-slightly-rolled-off above \|F\| ≈ 0.9**. The differences (0.005–0.018 rad/m) are below the 16-world cell sd (0.040), but they appear **symmetrically on both sides**, which argues they are real endpoint saturation rather than noise. Sign and magnitude are still maximal there; nothing inverts. Practically: commanding beyond ~0.9 buys no extra curvature.

**(b) A systematic mid-range left bias, marginally over my own bar.** The deterministic mirror offset `|κ(+F)| − |κ(−F)|` is a smooth hump — +0.036, +0.041, +0.039, +0.056, +0.088, +0.096, **+0.104**, +0.065, +0.022, +0.004, +0.017 across \|F\| = 0.05 → 1.00 — peaking at **0.1041 rad/m at \|F\| = 0.60**, against the **0.10 bar I predeclared**. That is a miss by 4 %, at one of eleven points, deterministic only; the stochastic peak is 0.0646 and the 32×5 offsets are 0.0011–0.0761, all inside. **I am not moving the bar.** The shape — vanishing at both ends, peaking mid-range — is consistent with the known constant pelvis-roll bias accepted as this plant's baseline. **Joe should watch for a slight left lean on mid arcs during visual review.**
