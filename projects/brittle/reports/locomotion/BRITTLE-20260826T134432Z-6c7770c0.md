## 4 · Why: the corridor is hold shaping, not acquisition shaping

Corridor payout `g` at the ratchet band [0.3693, 0.3893], σ 0.02:

| stride | g |
|---|---|
| 0.3209 (DR06H1 realized) | **0.0029** |
| 0.3275 (DR05D realized) | 0.0127 |
| 0.3334 (DR06G realized) | 0.0399 |
| 0.3693 (band edge) | 1.0000 |

At a ~0.05 m deficit the Gaussian shoulder has decayed to ~0.3 % of full payout, and its slope with it. The term therefore contributes almost no gradient from where the policy actually walks — it is designed to **hold** a stride once inside the band (flat top, so longer strides earn nothing extra), not to **pull** one up from outside. With the corridor silent, the remaining objectives did what they already wanted: a little more cadence, a little less stride. Nothing was gamed; the term simply had no voice.

This is a property of the term's shape, not of the per-member generalization, which is verifiably correct (§2). The plumbing you authorized works; what it revealed is that this particular term cannot acquire.
