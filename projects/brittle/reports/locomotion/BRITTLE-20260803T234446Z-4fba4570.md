## 2. Per-gate rejection audit — which thresholds reject visually valid walking

Ground truth = oracle visual detector (foot z > ground+8 mm ≥ 3 frames, ≥ 40 mm travel,
contact confirmation — the rocking-incident-hardened definition). Every visual step graded
against each gate:

| gate | threshold | s2-final measured | rejects visually-valid steps? |
|---|---|---|---|
| swing duration | ≥ 0.15 s | CPU 0.300 s med; GPU force-air 0.320 s med (96% pass) | no |
| clearance | ≥ 21 mm (0.7 × 30 mm) | apex 34.5 mm med (95% pass) | no |
| alternation | chain | 23 (CPU) / 42 (GPU) unbroken | no |
| stance retention / slide (lurch) | ≤ 10 mm | 2.3 mm med | no |
| contact chatter | run-length filters | **no chatter exists** — force stances are clean single segments; the ~2600 "edges" I called chatter in C11S5 = the steps themselves (edge count ≈ visual step count) | no |
| **stance duration** | **≥ 0.35 s (`rp_stance_min_t`), measured on the force channel** | **GPU force-loaded stance med 0.340 s, p90 0.340 — 0% of 2630 touchdowns pass; CPU z-channel stance 0.360 s passes 20/23** | **YES — the sole rejector** |

**Mechanism, fully measured:** the policy walks at double the book's cadence target, so its
force-loaded stance settles at ~0.34 s — **10 ms under the 0.35 s bar**. The kernel real-step
gate (`step_ok`) therefore rejected essentially every real step on the GPU → the real-step ET
clock starved → `et_no_step` (4.5 s) **killed walking robots at step 225 throughout training
and my battery** → "alt-chain 0 / stall 64/64 / young deaths" in the C11S5 report. The late
checkpoints' "clock farming" was the policy **adapting to the mismeasuring gate**: ~7
stances/world stretched past 0.35 s to reset the clock, extending episodes to 688 steps.
The z-channel vs force-channel stance gap (~20–40 ms: a foot is down-but-lightly-loaded at
stance edges) is why the same thresholds pass on CPU-z and fail on GPU-force. My C11S5
deep-eval qualifier inherited the same force-window stance_min and repeated the misgrade
(oracle-grades-outcome-not-mechanism, now at the measurement-channel level). Process note:
the standing walking-oracle rule ("run the oracle before filing reports") was not followed for
the C11S5 report — its z-based detector would have caught this before the wrong verdict shipped.

## 4. Minimum oracle correction (returned, NOT applied)

**Change one constant: `rp_stance_min_t` 0.35 → 0.30 s** (kernel real-step gate = the stall
clock's and cadence section's step qualifier). Everything else stays (clearance 21 mm, swing
0.15 s, travel, alternation, slide, ep-len gates).

Validation (`c11s5a_grade.py` + synthetic probes):

| trajectory | qualified steps @0.35 (today) | @0.30 (corrected) |
|---|---|---|
| s2-final GPU cohort (walking) | ~0 (7 clock-grazes/world) | **2418 = 37.8/world (of 42 visual)** |
| pure slide (never unloads) | 0 | **0** |
| standing | 0 | **0** |
| 60 ms load-chatter, no lift | 0 | **0** (clearance+air+stance all still reject) |

Skating remains inadmissible: a slide produces no unload events, so it can never produce a
touchdown, whatever stance_min is; the clearance (21 mm), swing (0.15 s), and travel gates are
untouched. Sensitivity: 0.30 → 92% qualify, 0.32 → 91%, 0.34 → 54% (cliff at the gait's
operating point) — 0.30 s sits safely off the cliff while keeping chatter rejection.
The walking oracle script itself needs no change (its z-detector already recognizes the gait).
