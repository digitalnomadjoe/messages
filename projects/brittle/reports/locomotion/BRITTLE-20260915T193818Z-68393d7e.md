## 1. The stance-height constraint — solved exactly, and what it costs

**Family A (ticket-exact).** With q_ref, the pelvis attitude and the foot orientation fixed, the hip-above-sole height is affine in the two spans: `H(u,l) = H0 + cU·(u−u0) + cL·(l−l0)`. Measured on probe plants (the map is linear, so finite differences are exact): **cU = +0.44609, cL = +0.93922** (world-z components of the thigh and shank directions at q_ref — the thigh is 63° from vertical so it buys little height). Hence for each thigh span u the shank is
**l(u) = 220.00 − 0.47496·(u − 130.00) mm**, a one-dimensional family through the canonical geometry. Every generated plant was re-measured: **stance-height residual ≤ 0.0005 mm on all 15 candidates** (target ≤ 2 mm), knee delta **0.000°** (q_ref untouched).

**Why this is not a free lunch (§1 "different mathematical treatment").** At fixed joint angles the ankle position under the hip is also affine in (u, l): the family moves the feet **1.051 mm REARWARD per mm of thigh** while the whole-body CoM moves only 0.27 mm/mm, so the CoM walks toward the toe edge: d_front shrinks from +42.0 mm (dU00) to +26.4 (dU20), +10.8 (dU40), −4.8 (dU60). The zero-action PD hold of Q_DEFAULT — the operating point every frozen policy assumes — sags into 8 sole contacts at dU20 and **falls forward from dU25 on** (§2 table). Exact q_ref + exact height therefore implies a toe-loaded stance; this is a kinematic identity, not a tuning artefact.

**Family B (the explicit alternative, analysis only).** Keeping the knee angle EXACT and additionally requiring the ankle to stay at its canonical (x, z) under the hip (so the CoM-in-polygon operating point is preserved) needs one more degree of freedom: the leg rotates as a whole. Solving (l, hip_pitch) with ankle_pitch = −(hip_pitch + knee) (foot flat) gives, per mm of thigh, **hip_pitch −0.25° and ankle_pitch +0.25°** and a steeper shank trade (dl/du ≈ −0.7 … −0.9); the zero-action stance then stands on all of dU05…dU70 (lean 5.4→8.0°, τmax ≤ 2.1 N·m, `data/family_B.json`). Family B is a q_ref RETARGET (hip and ankle, not the knee) and therefore changes the interface every frozen policy is bound to; the ticket forbids that for the retention runs, so **all screens and batteries below run family A**, and family B is reported for the owner as the geometry that would have to accompany any span change if the operating point is to be preserved.
