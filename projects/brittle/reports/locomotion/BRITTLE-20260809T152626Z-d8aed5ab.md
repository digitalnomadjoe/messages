### ⚠ What actually happened to the cadence curve

The slope is not the whole story, and the cell-by-cell delta says something the slope hides:

| c_cmd | baseline (init ckpt) | after 884,736 steps | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | 1.9122 | 1.9774 | **+0.0652** |
| 0.85 | 1.6105 | 1.6691 | **+0.0586** |
| 1.00 | 1.0901 | 1.1630 | **+0.0729** |

**The entire curve translated upward by an almost uniform +0.065 Hz.** The policy learned to step
*faster everywhere* — it did not learn to step faster *when asked to*. A uniform shift changes the
intercept, not the slope, which is exactly why the slope moved only +0.0255 while every cell moved
~2.5× that.

That is the **command-blindness signature** this programme has hit before: the term was paid down
by a global change in gait rather than by conditioning on `cadence_cmd`. The reward was reduced;
the *command channel* was not learned. Nominal speed rising 0.1638 → 0.1743 is the same effect
seen in the speed axis.

**Against the noise floor:** the 3-seed spread previously measured on this checkpoint family was
sd 0.0197, full range 0.0394. **+0.0255 sits inside that range**, so the honest statement is that
the slope did not move — not that it moved a little.
