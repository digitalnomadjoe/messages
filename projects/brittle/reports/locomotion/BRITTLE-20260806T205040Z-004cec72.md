## 0. Headline — the Q3 premise was false

**★★★ The Q3 PPO run never presented the turn commands to the policy.** The trainer's own `ADV_AUDIT` dump of the live rollout (258,048 samples, the exact batch the update would have used) contains:

| obs45 value | samples | share |
|---|---|---|
| **0.000** | **258,039** | **99.997 %** |
| −0.306 | 7 | 0.003 % |
| +0.306 | 2 | 0.001 % |

obs53 (cadence/2) is 0.5 on **100 %** of samples, so the observation wrap is applied every step — the **command channel specifically** is dead, not the wrap. Two left samples and seven right samples out of a quarter-million.

**Mechanism, localized (Part B0).** The turn command is written from the member's `yaw_cmd_profile`, which is non-zero only inside the curved zone. The three members' zones start at concat phase **213.6 / 861.6 / 1509.6** — i.e. **~214 control steps into each member's lead-in**. Measured directly: in the 64 steps after reset the fraction of samples with |obs45| > 0.05 is **0.00000**; after warming 300 steps it is **0.66667** — exactly the two turn members out of three, as designed. Q3 trained with `--max-steps 660`, `--arc-rsi 0.85` and a first-iteration `ep_len` of **129.9**, so episodes reset before ever reaching the zone.

Everything Q3 concluded about a left/right trade rests on a run in which neither turn was ever commanded. **The "capacity/representation limit" reading I gave in Q3 is therefore not supported**, and three further independent measurements below say the same thing.

★ **My Q3 preflight checked the right property in the wrong place.** Item 2 verified occupancy and printed `obs45_by_member {straight: 0.0, left: 0.306, right: −0.306}` — but that was computed from **member metadata** (`vx × kappa`), not from the live training observation stream. Occupancy was correct (1344/1344/1344); the observation was not. A preflight that reads the command from the buffer the optimizer will actually consume would have caught this before the run.

## 2. Part B — per-command PPO gradient audit

The trainer's live batch cannot answer items 1–4 (2 left and 7 right samples), so gradients were computed on **matched frozen batches** in which the command channel is verified live: 384 worlds × 64 steps warmed 300 steps into the zone, **8192 samples per command, matched exactly**, GAE(0.99, 0.95), clip 0.2, ratio ≡ 1 at the seed.

**Items 1–2 — pairwise cosine and norms (policy objective):**

| pair | cosine |
|---|---|
| straight vs left | **−0.103** |
| straight vs right | **−0.088** |
| **left vs right** | **−0.047** |

Norms 2.069 / 2.242 / 2.518. **These are near-orthogonal, not opposed.** A left-vs-right cosine of −0.047 is not destructive interference; it is what two mostly-independent objectives look like.

**Item 4 — term decomposition.** Policy objective norms as above. **Entropy gradient w.r.t. every actor-mean parameter is exactly 0.000** — the Normal's entropy depends only on the state-independent `log_std`, so the entropy term cannot participate in any left/right trade. **Anchor gradient is also exactly 0.000 at the seed**, because the anchor policy *is* the initialization (μ − μ_anchor ≡ 0); the `equal_mixed` and `equal_mixed_no_anchor` shadow steps are bit-identical, confirming it. **The anchor is not responsible for the trade.**

**Item 3 — localization.** Per layer, left-vs-right cosine: trunk.0 −0.040, trunk.2 −0.003, action_net.weight −0.050, action_net.bias −0.093; value net exactly 0 (the policy loss does not touch the critic). Per output joint the picture is **mixed-sign, not systematically opposed** — aligned on r_knee_pitch **+0.569**, r_hip_pitch +0.475, l_hip_yaw +0.342, l_hip_pitch +0.340, r_hip_roll +0.291; opposed on l_hip_roll **−0.611**, l_knee_pitch −0.425, l_ankle_pitch −0.287, r_ankle_roll −0.244. Real per-joint conflict exists on the roll/ankle group but is cancelled in aggregate by alignment elsewhere.

**Item 5 — per-command advantage.** straight mean 169.47 sd 56.18 · left **196.44** sd 70.47 · right 175.37 sd 57.30; clip fraction 0 at the seed by construction (ratio ≡ 1).

**Item 6 — the critic uses the signed command.** Flipping the sign of obs45 moves the value by **0.0952** on average against a value sd of 0.2964 — **32 % of a standard deviation**; zeroing it moves 0.0531. The critic is command-aware and healthy.

**★ Item 7 — mixed-batch advantage normalization IS asymmetric.** Under the single global normalization Q3 used, the per-command normalized advantage means are **straight −0.175, left +0.255, right −0.081** — a **+0.336 systematic bias toward left over right**, on a batch with exactly equal counts. Equal *world counts* do not equalize gradient contribution. The trainer already ships the fix — `--adv-norm-per-regime`, which normalizes "INDEPENDENTLY within kappa=0/+k/−k strata … Counters the per-side advantage-magnitude imbalance that count-balancing alone cannot fix" — and **Q3 did not enable it**. This is a real defect of the Q3 configuration, but it is *not* what produced the Q3 outcome, because Q3 had no live commands for it to act on.

**Shadow-step causality probes** (disposable deep copies only, lr 3e-4, one step; no shadow policy saved):

| probe | Δaction straight | Δaction left | Δaction right |
|---|---|---|---|
| left-only | 0.000688 | **0.000980** | 0.000531 |
| right-only | 0.000663 | 0.000683 | **0.001148** |
| equal mixed | 0.000293 | 0.000323 | 0.000366 |
| equal mixed, no anchor | 0.000293 | 0.000323 | 0.000366 |

- **Left-only improvement does not damage right** — it moves right *least* of the three (0.000531).
- **Right-only does not damage left** — symmetric (left 0.000683 < right 0.001148).
- **The equal mixed update attenuates rather than selects**: every per-command delta drops to ~0.0003, roughly a third of the single-command deltas, which is what near-orthogonal-to-mildly-negative gradients produce. It does not pick a side.
- **The anchor is not responsible**: no-anchor is bit-identical.
- Short deterministic rollouts after one step are unchanged to three decimals (net yaw 1.597 / 1.812 / 1.253 in every probe), i.e. one small step is far below behavioural resolution — reported so the null is not mistaken for evidence.

## 4. Why the decision is `CAPACITY LIMIT NOT SUPPORTED`

- `POLICY-GRADIENT INTERFERENCE CONFIRMED` — **not supported.** Left-vs-right cosine −0.047 (near-orthogonal); per-joint conflict is mixed-sign; single-command steps do not damage the other side; the mixed step attenuates rather than selects.
- `CRITIC OR ADVANTAGE DEFECT CONFIRMED` — the critic is **healthy** (uses the signed command at 32 % of value sd). A genuine advantage-normalization asymmetry exists (+0.336 left-over-right), and it should be fixed — but it demonstrably did **not** cause the Q3 result, since Q3 presented no commands for it to bias. Returning it would misattribute cause.
- `GATE RESULT STATISTICALLY UNRESOLVED` — **half-true only**, so not returnable as the single answer: Q2's *left* gate is unresolved (CI [0.9835, 1.0035]), but Q2's *right* gate is a **clear, larger-than-reported miss** (CI entirely above −1.0, ≈ −0.932).
- `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` — no refusal, no missing authorization; the unit ran end to end.
- **`CAPACITY LIMIT NOT SUPPORTED`** — every leg of the capacity story fails: the run never presented the commands (99.997 % obs45 = 0); on correctly-commanded batches the per-command gradients are near-orthogonal; the critic uses the command; the mixed update attenuates rather than selects; and the anchor contributes exactly zero. The Q3 anti-correlation is consistent with drift in a run where neither turn was commanded, not with a representational ceiling.

**Smallest causal intervention, recommended in this order:**

1. **Make the command live during training** — the one change that addresses the measured cause. The command turns on ~214 frames into each member and episodes reset before reaching it, so the minimal fix is to start episodes *inside* the curved zone (phase-offset the reset / scope arc-RSI injection to the member's zone) and **verify it from the optimizer's own batch** — `ADV_AUDIT_OUT` already dumps obs45, so the check is one assert: the share of |obs45| > 0.05 must be ≈ 2/3. No architecture, reward, gate or law change.
2. **Then** enable `--adv-norm-per-regime`, which removes the measured +0.336 normalized-advantage asymmetry that would otherwise bias the first genuinely-commanded run toward left.
3. **Re-cut nothing yet.** Q2's right-stochastic gate is a real 0.068 miss and its left is unresolved; both should be re-measured at 32 worlds × 5 reps *after* (1) and (2), because every earlier 16-world number in this arc is now known to be under-powered.
