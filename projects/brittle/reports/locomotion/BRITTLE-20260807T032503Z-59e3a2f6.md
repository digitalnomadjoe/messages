## 0. Headline

**The ~2 % curvature deficit does not close without more torque. It is the torque.**

The unit asked one question and got a clean answer. Q8C bought curvature with torque-burst growth. Q8D repriced that burst **30×** — every other byte of construction held fixed — and ran the same 2,064,384 steps from the same checkpoint. The result:

| over 2,064,384 steps, same construction, same budget, same start | Q8C (`2,8,4`) | **Q8D (`2,240,120`)** |
|---|---|---|
| `kappa_floor` trend | 0.942 → **1.027 peak** → 1.006 | **0.984 peak at the FIRST checkpoint** → 0.895 |
| `kappa_floor` at 32×5 | **0.9831** | **0.9408** |
| on-distribution duty > 8 N·m over the run | rose | **rose +20.9 %** |
| on-distribution duty > 10 N·m over the run | rose | **rose +24.0 %** |

**The 30× price did not stop the policy taking the torque — duty above 10 N·m still rose 24 % — and the curvature, which in Q8C rose in exactly that window, instead fell.** Charging for the burst removed the gain and left the burst. That is a Pareto limit, measured rather than argued.

## 1. The offline income audit (the ticket's first required step)

Predeclared in `q8d_audit_criteria.json` + addendum **before** any measurement, executed by `q8d_audit.py` / `q8d_select.py` / `q8d_finalize.py`, both policies under the deployed actor `g`, income read from the **component buffer** (`sim._comps_buf`) and duty from `sim.get_tau_stats()` — the same call `--torque-cost` consumes.

★ **At `2,8,4` the torque objective is measurement, not shaping.** The *entire* term costs **0.0089 per control step against a measured 17.54 per-step reward — 0.051 %.**

★ **The Q8B→Q8C trade was bought at a 49× discount.** Over that interval the policy **gained 0.1233/step of income** and **paid 0.0025/step of torque cost**. The burst growth was, to a first approximation, free.

⚠ **The turn channels did not pay for the curvature.** The predeclared "prize" — the sum over `r_yaw`, `r_yaw_integ`, `r_yaw_rate`, `r_step_yaw`, `r_wrongway`, `p_wrongdir` — came out **negative** (−0.0041; `r_yaw` alone −0.0041). The sim-side book *fell* by 0.045 while total reward rose 0.127, so the entire gain came from the ReftrackAdapter's tracking terms, which are not in the component buffer. Reported, not smoothed over: it means the predeclared materiality test fell through to its fallback branch.

**No weight set satisfies materiality and non-domination together.** Making the burst growth genuinely material needs scale **99×–479×** depending on which weights move; there the penalty's P99.9 reaches **260–606 per step** against a 17.5/step mean income and its standard deviation is **4.6× the reward's**. That is torque dominating advantage, which the ticket forbids. The predeclared no-solution branch fired: **price as hard as possible under the domination caps.**

| candidate | D-cap scale | Δprice at cap | mean cost | P99.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `w10` only | 32.0 | 0.0137 | 0.33 % of R | 17.47 |
| **`w10`+`wema`** | **30.5** | **0.0546** | **1.11 % of R** | **17.44** |
| `w10`+`wema`+`w8` | 23.5 | 0.0591 | 1.19 % of R | 17.31 |

The all-three rung prices 8 % harder by multiplying the broadest term 20×; the predeclared priority ranks `w8` **last**, so the tie broke to `w10`+`wema`, rounded down to a clean **×30**.

**Chosen: `--torque-cost 2,240,120`** — `w10` and `wema` ×30, `w8` untouched. Calibrated to the on-distribution duty: mean cost 1.10 % of reward, the Q8B→Q8C burst growth now costs **21.4× its base price**, D1–D5 all satisfied. Receipt `eeb5f96b5ab04fac`, pinned inside the launcher card and asserted at launch.

⚠ **There is no `>11` weight and none was added** (that would be reward redesign). `>11` is covered transitively: `d10` counts every sample above 10 N·m, so every `>11` sample is already inside it.

⚠ **The audit predicted its own insufficiency.** Even at the cap the price (0.0546) is below the prize (0.1233) — it makes the trade ~44 % less profitable, not unprofitable. I said so before the run rather than after.

## 7. Why the decision

- `SYMMETRIC MANIFOLD READY FOR VISUAL REVIEW` — not returnable under C6: both stochastic gates fail and the burst requirement fails. Nothing staged.
- `TORQUE-CONSTRAINED PPO IMPROVES BUT REMAINS SUB-GATE` — **false**, and this is the point. It did not improve. `kappa_floor` peaked at the first checkpoint and fell to 0.8950; at 32×5 it went 0.9831 → 0.9408.
- `STRAIGHT OR ROBUSTNESS REGRESSED` — **false**. Straight is clean at 32×5 (+0.0212 / +0.0069, STRAIGHT 32/32, 0/160 falls) and robustness is 4/480, in family with the whole arc. The per-checkpoint `straight |κ|` column is a worst-world statistic and does not support this reading.
- `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` — no refusal, no missing authorization; the Guard refusals encountered were correct and were satisfied, not worked around.
- **`CURVATURE-TORQUE PARETO LIMIT CONFIRMED`** — the two quantities moved against each other exactly as a Pareto frontier requires. Q8C: burst up, curvature up. Q8D at 30× the price: curvature down, and the duty the price targeted **still rose 24 %**. The deficit is not slack the optimizer failed to find; the torque is how the curvature is produced.

**Recommendation.** The manifold sits at `kappa_floor` **0.9831** (Q8C, unpriced) or **0.9408** (Q8D, priced), against a ±1.0 gate, exactly symmetric, deterministic curvature clearing 1.0 on both sides, the lowest fall rates in the arc — and it cannot have both the last ~2 % and the Q2 burst envelope. Three options I can distinguish, in the order I would take them:

**(i) Re-cut the stochastic gate at the measured achievable band** — now the best-supported option, because this unit removes the "it just needs the right price" hypothesis that made (i) premature in Q8C. Deterministic curvature is comfortably past ±1.0; the shortfall is confined to σ=0.135 exploration noise. **Joe's call — deliberately out of scope here.**

**(ii) Attack the torque at its source rather than its price** — the burst is concentrated where curvature is produced, so the lever is mechanical (reference curvature profile, cadence, or step geometry at the turn), not a reward weight. That is a new unit with its own ticket, not a continuation.

**(iii) Accept Q8C's `13fc030e59ddfc65` as the best available and treat the Q2 burst envelope as the thing to renegotiate** — coherent, but it changes a stated requirement and is squarely Joe's.

**Not recommended:** more steps at either weight set, or a torque price between the two. This unit brackets the trade from both ends.

**Nothing is banked, promoted or pointed at. No visual sign-off is recorded — the inspector ran, but I am not Joe's eyes.**
