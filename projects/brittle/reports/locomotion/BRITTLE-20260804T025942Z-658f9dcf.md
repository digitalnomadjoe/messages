Full q/foot/phase/path-anchor captured once; `track = 16·exp(−Σ i_k(σ)·err_k²)` re-priced offline at the anneal points (every component kernel ∝ 1/σ² — one recording prices all σ). ## 5. Questions answered → decision

1. **Does final-book track pay walking more than standing?** NO — the inversion persists at every anneal point (both →0 at 100%).
2. **Does it distinguish c344's phase-locked pelvis from C11S5?** Only as a 16-vs-0 cliff at the reference itself; no gradient in between (§4).
3. **Is a separate R5 pelvis term still necessary?** **YES.** Exact missing component: an **ADDITIVE, phase-locked pelvis term — lateral position + roll, two axes** — `r_pelvis_phase = w·[exp(−(y−y_ref(φ))²/2σ_y²) + exp(−(roll−roll_ref(φ))²/2σ_roll²)]` with σ at ACHIEVABLE scale (σ_y ≈ 25 mm, σ_roll ≈ 3°). Additivity is the load-bearing property: per-axis improvements pay independently of joint/feet saturation — precisely what the product kernel destroys. (This also subsumes the C11S6-measured lateral-family inversions: phase-locked deviation, not magnitude, is the discriminator.)
4. **Did the 48M ceiling end too early for the intended final economy?** The ceiling ended mid-anneal (52%), but running longer would have made things WORSE, not better: the σ-schedule outran the achievable error scale (errors did not shrink with σ), so the final book's track is an income cliff paying nobody. The anneal ENDPOINT is mis-designed for this error regime — a σ-floor at the achievable scale (or error-adaptive scheduling) belongs in the R6 discussion.
5. **Would s2-final be a valid warm start under the final book?** Mechanically yes (its pelvis path error ~5 cm survives even the final 0.15 m err-ET band; obs/action/plant/law compatible) — but it would train **track-blind** (≈0 income, ≈0 gradient); everything would ride on the step economy + r_fwd. Valid, with that caveat explicit.

**DECISION: FAIL** (per the ticket's rule) — the missing component is returned above for a narrowly scoped R5 design. Nothing was modified.
