## 0. Headline — the two findings that shape the whole plan

**(1) The observation contract already carries all three commands. No contract change is required.**
`obs45` = yaw-rate command, `obs52` = `vx_cmd`, `obs53` = `cadence/2` are all present in the 54-D actor
contract, and the manifold resolver already builds the full `(vx × cadence × ±κ)` cross product. **What
is missing is the reference family, not the code or the contract.**

**(2) But speed and cadence are today a single degenerate axis, and the cadence channel is INVERTED.**
Measured closed-loop by overriding each channel in the observation the actor consumes:

| override | realized speed | realized cadence | **stride = v/c** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `obs52` (vx_cmd) 0.100 → 0.240 | 0.177 → **0.227** ✔ right way | 1.562 → 0.714 | **0.113 → 0.318** |
| `obs53` (cadence) 0.85 → 1.20 Hz | 0.181 → 0.217 | **1.613 → 0.725** ✘ **backwards** | **0.112 → 0.299** |

★★ **Both channels move the same latent quantity — stride — over nearly the same range.** They are not
two axes; they are one. And commanding *more* cadence produces *less*: ask for 0.85 Hz and you get
1.613 Hz; ask for 1.20 Hz and you get 0.725 Hz. Speed authority exists and is correctly signed but has
low gain (a 2.4× command change buys a 1.28× speed change).

This is exactly what one should expect: `obs52` and `obs53` were **perfectly constant** through every
unit that produced this checkpoint — 1 distinct value each across 6,400 sampled observations. The
channels are live wires with no learned semantics. **Breaking that degeneracy is the entire job of the
acquisition unit, and it dictates the lattice in Part D.**

## 2. Part B — the minimum physical command vector

```
u = [ v_cmd , kappa_cmd , c_cmd ]
```

| component | physical meaning | unit | initial range | obs channel & normalisation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `v_cmd` | commanded forward **path** speed (along the arc, not straight-line) | m·s⁻¹ | **0.10 → 0.24** | `obs52 = v_cmd` (raw m/s) |
| `kappa_cmd` | commanded **signed path curvature**, + = left | rad·m⁻¹ | **−2.0 → +2.0** | `obs45 = v_cmd · kappa_cmd` (yaw rate, rad/s) |
| `c_cmd` | commanded **same-foot** cadence (one full cycle of one foot) | Hz | **0.85 → 1.20** | `obs53 = c_cmd / 2` |

Derived, not commanded: `stride = v_cmd / c_cmd` (0.083 → 0.282 m over the box), `step_rate = 2·c_cmd`.

**Ranges justified from measurement, not taste.** `v` upper bound 0.24 — the closed-loop override
already reached 0.227 m/s with 0/16 falls; lower bound 0.10 is below the banked 0.153 with margin.
`c` 0.85–1.20 brackets the banked 1.0 and both realized extremes seen in the probes (0.714–1.613 Hz).
`κ` ±2.0 is the banked capability; note it **saturates above ~0.9 of full command**, so usable authority
is ≈ ±1.8 rad/m.

★ **Independence is a property of the reference family, not of the retiming.** A pure time-rescale of one
gait locks `v = stride · c` with stride fixed, so `v` and `c` cannot move independently. The existing
`ccref4b` generator already breaks this — at fixed `v = 0.29`, members `c0p880 / c1p000 / c1p150` carry
`loop_xadv` 1.648 / 1.450 / 1.261, i.e. the **spatial step geometry changes**, not just the playback
rate. Part D reuses that property; it does not rely on retiming.

**Xbox mapping** (consistent with the existing MVP calibration, deadzone 0.15):

| control | command | mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Left stick **Y** | `v_cmd` | 0.10 → 0.24 m/s, centre-detent at the banked 0.153 |
| Left stick **X** | `kappa_cmd` | ±2.0 rad/m, centre = straight, ±0.05 deadband (the Q10 predeclared near-zero band) |
| Right stick **Y** *(or LT/RT pair)* | `c_cmd` | 0.85 → 1.20 Hz, centre-detent at 1.00 |
| **A** (hold) | deadman | existing march-demo convention |

Rate-limit every channel at the source (the `CommandSafetyLayer` already specified for the SE(3) ladder):
`v` ≤ 0.15 m/s², `κ` ≤ 2.0 rad/m/s, `c` ≤ 0.5 Hz/s — the measured command-response latency is 40 ms, so
these are far inside the actor's bandwidth.

## 3. Part C — observation contract verdict

### **VERDICT: NO OBSERVATION CHANGE REQUIRED. Nothing to approve, nothing to implement.**

| check | result |
|---|---|
| do all three channels exist in the 54-D contract? | **yes** — `obs45`, `obs52`, `obs53` |
| does the trainer's resolver support a 3-D `(v, c, ±κ)` manifold? | **yes** — `combos = [(v,c) for v in vset for c in cset]`, then `±κ` per combo, fail-closed on unpaired members. **No code change.** |
| were the channels exercised in the banked artifact? | **no** — `obs52` and `obs53` each had **1 distinct value** across 6,400 observations (`0.1530`, `0.5000`, std 0.0000) |
| is the actor blind to them? | **no, but worse than blind** — see below |

**Open-loop sensitivity** (sweeping each channel on 6,400 real observations): `obs45` moves the action by
137 % of the mean |action| (positive control); `obs52` by 34 %; `obs53` by 42 %. So the weights are live.

**Closed-loop authority** is the number that matters, and it is where the problem is (§0): speed tracks
weakly but correctly; **cadence tracks backwards**; and both channels are collinear on stride.

⚠ **One semantic wart I recommend keeping.** `obs45` carries `v·κ` (a yaw *rate*), not `κ`. Once speed
varies, the same `obs45` means different curvature. This is **recoverable inside the existing contract**
— `κ = obs45 / obs52`, and both are present — so I recommend **keeping the contract unchanged**. Changing
`obs45` to carry `κ` directly would be a contract break that invalidates the banked actor and forces a
retrain for no measured benefit. **Flagged as a decision, with a recommendation, not implemented.**

## 4. Part D — acquisition plan

### 4.1 The lattice is designed around one test: iso-stride disambiguation

Because the actor collapses `(v, c)` onto stride, the family must contain cells that **share a stride but
differ in `(v, c)`**. If the policy can only read stride, those cells are indistinguishable to it, and the
training signal is forced to break the degeneracy. Inside the box, stride 0.176 m gives a clean triple:

```
(v=0.153, c=0.87)   (v=0.176, c=1.00)   (v=0.200, c=1.14)      all stride ≈ 0.176 m
```

### 4.2 Smallest training family — 13 members

| # | `v` | `c` | `κ` | stride | role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 0.153 | 1.00 | 0, +2, −2 | 0.153 | **centre + curvature retention** (these three exist today as `family_q8d`) |
| 4 | 0.110 | 1.00 | 0 | 0.110 | speed axis, low |
| 5 | 0.200 | 1.00 | 0 | 0.200 | speed axis, high |
| 6 | 0.153 | 0.85 | 0 | 0.180 | cadence axis, low |
| 7 | 0.153 | 1.15 | 0 | 0.133 | cadence axis, high |
| 8–10 | 0.176 | 1.00 | 0, +2, −2 | 0.176 | **iso-stride partner + curvature at a non-nominal speed** |
| 11–13 | 0.200 | 1.14 | 0, +2, −2 | 0.176 | **iso-stride partner + curvature at non-nominal (v, c)** |

Seven distinct `(v, c)` cells; nine `κ = 0` members plus curvature at three of them. Curvature is placed
**on the iso-stride diagonal** deliberately — that is where the degeneracy lives and where Part A showed
curvature already moves cadence by 39 %, so it is the cell that tests all three couplings at once.

### 4.4 Reference construction

**A new family must be generated — this is the real prerequisite.** Today: `family_q8d` is a single
`(0.153, 1.00)` point with `±κ`; `ccref4b` has a genuine 4 × 4 `(v, c)` grid but at **vx 0.24–0.32** and
with **zero curved variants** (`grep -c "_kP\|_kM"` → 0). **The intersection of "curved" and "non-nominal
(v, c)" is empty.** Generate the 13 members with the existing `rl/scripts/launch_ccref_grid.sh` at the
curvature operating point, honouring `stride = v/c` (not a retime), emitting the resolver's expected
names `ccref_vx{v*100:03d}_c{c:.3f→p}[_kP200|_kM200].npz`, each passing its own DFL preamble.

### 4.5 RSI / reset and balance

Inherit the Q5A construction unchanged: **P28/P28b initial in-zone reset** (each world seeded inside its
own member's command zone, observation re-read post-injection — the stale-obs trap), **P27 zone
respawn**, and **per-regime advantage normalisation** extended from the 3-way `κ` partition to the
13-member partition. At 4032 worlds that is ~310 worlds/member, comfortably above the **64-sample
minimum** below which the normaliser silently falls back to global statistics.

### 4.6 Qualification criteria

1. **Speed tracking** — realized path speed within ±10 % of `v_cmd` at every train and held-out cell.
2. **Cadence tracking** — realized same-foot cadence within ±10 % of `c_cmd`, and **correctly signed**
   (today it is inverted; sign correctness is the primary gate, not the tolerance).
3. **Independence — the decisive gate.** Across the iso-stride triple, realized speeds must separate in
   the commanded order with non-overlapping CI95 at 32×5. *A policy reading only stride cannot pass this.*
4. **Curvature retention** — at the curvature cells, `κ` sign correct 100 %, magnitude within the Q10
   band, and no regression at the banked centre.
5. **Held-out interpolation** — all 8 held-out cells meet 1–3 without having been trained.
6. **Robustness** — zero deterministic falls; stochastic falls in family (≤ 4/480).
7. **Torque monitoring, not a gate** — per-joint P99 and 1 s-RMS P99 tracked against the Part A baseline
   and the preserved 12.5 N·m cap.
