- **Both stochastic falls are gone.** Stage A dropped one world of sixteen in *each* stochastic turn cell; the closure student drops **0/16 in all six cells**, at every snapshot from 200 through 1400.

## 1. Fixed inputs and the RSI method

Init **`2af78ef310197bde`** (Q1 Stage A) — not L2-final, not the Q1 Stage B student. Teachers straight `da70f57b4c5bae6b`, left `bab5bbb24c79cb47`, right `a64d283663bdec91`, all byte-verified before collection. Plant, walking law v2, 54-D contract, action scale 0.20 and oracle `c5cb35c05e62628f` unchanged.

★ **The ticket's "save teacher-orbit states, then RSI the student from them" cannot be done by state injection on this stack, and I measured why rather than assuming it.** `BrittleMjwBatchSimTip.set_state(reanchor=True)` is the only injection hook, and its own reset kernel **zeroes `prev_action` and restarts every touchdown/contact latch**; `reanchor=False` leaves the destination world's latches untouched, which is no better. Injecting a saved teacher state and reading the observation back gives **prev_action error 0.478** and **touchdown-timer error 0.66** — i.e. literal injection would have RSI'd the student into a state that is *not* the saved teacher state, silently corrupting dims 33–47 of every RSI point. (A third figure, joints 2.08, is **confounded** by the extra zero-action step the probe takes and is not evidence of injection loss; the prev_action and timer figures are, and they follow directly from the documented reset path.)

**RSI is therefore done by in-rollout handover:** the teacher acts to a per-world step `K_w = 60 + (w mod 50)`, at which point the state *is* a valid teacher-orbit state with every hidden latch consistent by construction, and the Stage A student takes over from exactly that state under σ=0.135 inside the same continuous episode. `K_w` spans a full 1.0 s gait cycle, giving **512 RSI points per command over 50 distinct phases** with stance identity split 244/268 (left) and 288/224 (right).

**Collection receipts.** 128 worlds × 700 steps × 4 seeds per turn command. Left **244,164** rows (15 falls), right **244,887** rows (19 falls); pre-failure rows (final 50 steps before termination) 700 and 950. Excluded: 4,222 / 3,433 post-terminal rows and 67,982 / 68,048 command-mismatched rows. ⚠ The invalid-contact filter (pelvis below `TIP_FALL_Z` 0.28) removed **0 additional rows** — the environment terminates at or before that criterion, so the post-terminal filter already subsumes it; reported rather than left as an unexamined zero. **Previous-action units verified exactly: max error 0.000, 0 bad rows.** Straight retention: 244,164 rows of valid Stage A student states labelled by the straight teacher, matched in size.

## 4. Teacher recovery from the collected pre-failure states — why imitation cannot finish

This is the measurement the ticket asked for, and it is the unit's most important result. Each fallen world was replayed with the **identical pre-generated noise**, handing control back to the **teacher** δ control-steps before the student's termination:

| handback point | left | right | **pooled** |
|---|---|---|---|
| δ = 10 (0.2 s before termination) | 0/6 | 0/8 | **0/14** |
| δ = 25 (0.5 s before) | 0/6 | 0/7 | **0/13** |
| δ = 50 (1.0 s before) | 2/3 | 4/4 | **6/7 (86 %)** |

**By half a second before termination the state is already unrecoverable for the teacher itself — 0 of 27 pooled.** The recoverable boundary sits between 0.5 s and 1.0 s out. Since the pre-failure window is 50 steps (1.0 s), roughly half of the rows that were oversampled to 25 % of every turn minibatch carry labels that are *not* recovery actions — they are a competent teacher's best effort in a state it also dies from. A quarter of the update's turn signal was therefore spent cloning failure.

That is a measured, mechanistic reason why one more imitation round is the wrong instrument: **in the region that actually kills the student there is no successful teacher behaviour to imitate.** Supplying a correct action there requires optimising against the *outcome*, which is what a bounded PPO robustness closure does and what BC/DAgger structurally cannot.

⚠ **Limitation, disclosed.** Paired-pass divergence at the handback step was **median 0.00079 (left) / 0.00020 (right)** — effectively identical states, which is what makes the comparison valid — but the **maxima were 2.45 and 3.14** across 15 and 19 worlds. A minority of worlds diverged materially before handback (dynamics near a fall are chaotic), so those individual verdicts describe a nearby state rather than the exact collected one. The conclusion survives it: the δ=10 and δ=25 results are **unanimous** across 27 worlds, and δ=50 is 6/7 the other way.

## 6. Why the decision is `TARGETED DAGGER MISSES — GPU PPO REQUIRED`

- `DETERMINISTIC SKILLS REGRESSED` — **false.** Deterministic curvature is within 2.2 %/4.9 % of Stage A against a 10 % gate, all six classification cells are 8/8, straight is untouched.
- `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` — **false.** No Guard refusal, no tooling refusal, no missing authorization; the unit ran end to end. (Two operational defects were mine and are disclosed in §7.)
- `TARGETED DAGGER CLOSES MANIFOLD` — **not returnable under C6.** Two stated Require-gates fail, however narrowly.
- `THREE-SKILL STUDENT READY FOR GPU MANIFOLD TRAINING` — **not returnable under C6** for the same reason; the gate list is the readiness bar.
- **`TARGETED DAGGER MISSES — GPU PPO REQUIRED`** — the measured outcome, and the ticket's own instruction for a miss.

**Recommendation, for your gate.** One bounded GPU PPO robustness closure seeded from **`b91ccf27ea5cbb88`** (snapshot 1400), on the three-command manifold, with the Q1/Q2 gate battery as the acceptance test and early-stop on first pass. The precedent is exact: in P1A the mirrored right student sat at 1/16 stochastic falls and κ −0.899 and a bounded PPO seed closed it at 524,288 steps to 0/16 and −1.011 without trading away the imitation prior. The student now handed over is in a **better** state than that one — 0/16 everywhere, both turns within 0.016 of gate — and the remaining deficit is a curvature magnitude that PPO's reward prices directly. PPO is prohibited in this unit; the launch is yours to authorize.

## 7. Two operational defects of mine, disclosed

1. **Artifact path rebind.** `q2_collect.py` reuses the Q1 head via `exec` to guarantee an identical environment rig, and that head **rebinds `A`** to the Q1 artifact directory — so the first collection wrote all six outputs into `c11s15q1_orbit_.../`. Caught on the completion check, files relocated unchanged, and both Q2 scripts now bind `A` *after* the exec. **No data was affected**; hashes are of the relocated files.
2. **Corrupted reference concat.** Stopping the first gate run killed the parent shell rather than the Python child, which kept running and left `probe_out/ref_concat.npz` half-written; the next run died on `BadZipFile: Bad CRC-32 for 'tpos.npy'`. Killed properly (split-pattern `pgrep`, per standing practice), cleared the directory and re-ran. Tier-1 results were cached, so **no measurement was repeated under different conditions and none was lost.**
