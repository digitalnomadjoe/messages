★★★ **And the deficit moves with the mirror.** The transformed policy's *left* is now the weak side — **+0.9356, CI [0.9071, 0.9640]** — the mirror image of the original's untrained-right value. **A world-side asymmetry could not do that**: if the plant, reference, observation or reward favoured left, mirroring the network would leave right deficient. Instead the weakness travelled with the weights. The deficit is a property of **what the optimizer found**, not of the side of the world it was asked to turn toward.

- **Second pass** injected `mirror(qpos, qvel)` into the right env and matched member-local phase. Step-level divergence then looked physically sane — first threshold crossings at **step 3 (joint qd 7.0e-3)**, **step 4 (observation 7.0e-3)**, **step 27 (joint q 1.1e-4)**, **step 73 (root height 1.2e-4)** — i.e. chaotic amplification of floating-point differences, not structure. **But its reward arm reported `track` income of 1065 (left) against 4.0 (right)**, which is not credible: an env whose tracking income is ~0 could not host the −1.150 curvature Part D measures in that same env. The injected right arm is mis-anchored, not asymmetric.

**I am therefore not asserting any Part B or C result.** The questions they were built to answer are bounded instead by Part D, which is the stronger instrument: an exactly-mirrored policy reproduces mirror-magnitude behaviour on the right to within 3–6 %, which no material plant, observation or reward asymmetry would permit. A correct paired-injection harness (anchoring the right arm through the same path the env uses at episode start, rather than by hand) is the outstanding work if a step-level bound is wanted.

## 5. Why the decision, and the smallest intervention

- `REFERENCE OR REWARD ASYMMETRY CONFIRMED` — the reference half is **positively excluded** (Part A, every field 0.000e+00); the reward half is not established either way, and Part D bounds any residual at 3–6 %.
- `PLANT OR OBSERVATION ASYMMETRY CONFIRMED` — **excluded by the strongest available evidence**: the mirrored policy clears the right gate in the same plant and observation contract, and the weakness follows the weights rather than the world.
- `RIGHT DEFICIT NOT EXPLAINED` — no longer true; it is explained as an optimization-path property.
- `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` — no refusal or missing authorization.
- **`MIRRORED LEFT POLICY PASSES RIGHT — OPTIMIZATION PATH ASYMMETRY`** — the plant and reference support the skill; right-side optimization from the Q2 initialization does not find it.

**Smallest intervention supported by the identified first asymmetry.** The asymmetry is in the *initialization/optimization path*, so the smallest fix does not involve training at all: **adopt the exact network mirror of the Q6 left finalist as the right-side policy and re-qualify it.** It already passes the right gates at 32×5 (det −1.158, stoch −1.032 with the CI entirely passing, zero deterministic falls). This is the same operator that produced the banked right specialist in P, now shown to also beat a full-budget from-scratch right run.

Two honest caveats on that recommendation: the transformed policy is a **right specialist** (its left is +0.9356), so it does not by itself close the three-command manifold; and its straight-stochastic falls are 15/160 versus 6/160 for the source. **Whether one network can hold both turns remains open** — but Q7 removes plant, reference and command-pathway asymmetry from the list of explanations, and points the next ticket at initialization: seed a manifold run from a *pair* of mirror-related competents rather than from a single straight-walker prior. **Nothing is banked, promoted or pointed at, and the transformed policy is retained only as disposable evidence.**
