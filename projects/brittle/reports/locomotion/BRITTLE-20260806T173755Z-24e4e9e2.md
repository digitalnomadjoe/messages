## 5. Why the decision is `STUDENT-STATE DAGGER REQUIRED`

By elimination against the fixed enum, on measured evidence:

- `DIRECT MLP STILL AVERAGES SKILLS` — **false.** One MLP carries straight, left and right simultaneously at teacher-level curvature with 8/8 classification and 8/8 sign on both turns.
- `STUDENT-STATE DAGGER CLOSES MANIFOLD` — **false.** One bounded round was run; it fixed one fall, lost the left κ gate, weakened right curvature and classification, and left the right-stochastic fall standing.
- `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` — **false.** No Guard refusal, no tooling refusal, no missing authorization; the unit executed end to end.
- `THREE-SKILL STUDENT READY FOR GPU MANIFOLD TRAINING` — **not returnable under C6.** Two of the ticket's Require-gates fail. The precedent is real and points the other way (P declared the mirrored right student ready for acquisition in a *worse* state — 1/16 falls, κ −0.899 — and a bounded PPO seed closed it), but a stated blocking gate is never overridden by precedent, so I am not returning READY on my own judgement.
- **`STUDENT-STATE DAGGER REQUIRED`** — what the evidence supports. Stage A does not clear the bar alone; the ticket's designated in-scope remedy is student-state DAgger; and the version I ran was a **simplified variant that omitted the specified teacher-orbit state initialization and failure-region targeting**, so the specified remedy is still outstanding.

**What I would do next, for your gate:** the residual is two worlds out of ninety-six under exploration noise, in a policy that is otherwise at teacher parity. Either (i) the DAgger the ticket actually specifies — student rollouts **RSI'd from saved valid teacher-orbit states inside the curved zone**, collected through the pre-failure region, so the new data concentrates where supervision is missing rather than where it already exists; or (ii) the bounded PPO robustness closure that resolved the identical gap in P1A at 524 k steps, which is prohibited here and needs your authorization. I recommend (i) first — it stays inside imitation, it is one bounded closure, and (ii) remains available if it misses.
