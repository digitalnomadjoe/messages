> Add `ccref_vx015_c0p700` (stride 0.2186) and `ccref_vx015_c0p850` (stride 0.1800) at **κ = 0,

**Confidence: HIGH on the diagnosis, MEDIUM on the repair being sufficient alone.**

sampling balance (10 / 55 / 35 %), not representation.

## 1. Command intervention — the actor is *sensitive* to obs53, but not *distinctly*

Fixed bank of **256 real walking observations** (nominal cell, post-warmup, fingerprint
`bank_fingerprint` in the receipt), held byte-identical while **only** the command column is
edited. Physical state, phase obs50/51 and everything else frozen. All three actors run as
deployed `g_v1` with the wrap asserted bound.

| actor | checkpoint | base ‖a‖ | **cadence ‖Δa‖** | speed ‖Δa‖ | curvature ‖Δa‖ | cad/speed | **cos(cad, speed)** | cos(cad, curv) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| banked seed | `13fc030e59ddfc65` | 0.6148 | **0.19274** | 0.06562 | 0.97561 | 2.94 | **+0.9126** | +0.0149 |
| pre-run CY | `307ea9190b5a941b` | 0.6063 | **0.18864** | 0.06369 | 0.98683 | 2.96 | **+0.8937** | +0.0154 |
| failed 884k | `3254cf0ff775fc2d` | 0.5942 | **0.18008** | 0.06129 | 0.97404 | 2.94 | **+0.8664** | +0.0166 |

**The actor is not blind to obs53.** A 0.70 → 1.00 cadence sweep moves the action by **0.180**,
which is **30.3 % of the base action norm**. Any verdict resting on "insensitive to obs53" is
wrong on the measurement.

**But the response is not a distinct channel.** `cos(cadence, speed) = +0.87…+0.91` — the
cadence-response and speed-response vectors are nearly **parallel**. For contrast, curvature is a
genuinely separate channel on the same network: `cos(cadence, curvature) = +0.015`, orthogonal,
with 5.4× the magnitude. The network is perfectly capable of holding distinct command axes; obs53
simply is not one of them — it is **aliased onto obs52**.

Response **coherence** (‖mean Δa‖ / mean ‖Δa‖) tells the same story: curvature **0.78**,
speed 0.53, **cadence 0.39**. The cadence response is the least consistent across states — a
state-dependent smear rather than a systematic command mapping.

### Did 884k increase cadence conditioning? **No — it slightly decreased it.**

`0.19274 → 0.18864 → 0.18008`, monotonically **down**: −6.6 % from the seed, **−4.5 % across the
884,736-step run**. The base action norm also fell (0.6148 → 0.5942). The run changed the base
gait and, if anything, *weakened* the obs53 response. It did not build a cadence channel.

## 2. Training-distribution identifiability — **the confound, exactly**

`stride_cmd = vx / c` for **all 20 members, zero violations**. Cadence is therefore not a free
command in this dataset — it is a ratio of the other two quantities.

**Only 2 of 18 `(vx, κ)` cells contain more than one cadence:**

| cell | cadences present |
|---|---|
| vx = 0.153, κ = −0.60 | {0.85, 1.00} |
| vx = 0.153, κ = +0.60 | {0.85, 1.00} |

**`c = 0.70` appears in no identifying cell at all.** Both c=0.70 members sit at κ = 0 with
unique speeds (0.107, 0.168), so *every* c=0.70 world can be reproduced from obs52 and obs45
alone, with obs53 never consulted.

**Sampling is also badly unbalanced** (world counts from the run's own regime strata):

| cadence | worlds | share |
|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | 231 | **10.0 %** |
| 0.85 | 1267 | **55.0 %** |
| 1.00 | 806 | 35.0 % |

### The confound predicts the inversion — including its sign

World-weighted over the actual training distribution:

| c_cmd | E[vx] | E[stride] |
|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | 0.1376 | 0.1965 |
| 0.85 | 0.1463 | 0.1721 |
| 1.00 | **0.1604** | 0.1604 |

`corr(c_cmd, vx) = +0.204`. **On this data, "higher obs53" genuinely means "faster".** So an actor
that maps obs53 onto speed is *correct on the marginal* — and that is exactly what the failed
checkpoint does at gate time:

| c_cmd | realized speed | realized cadence | realized stride |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | 0.1572 | 1.9774 | 0.0795 |
| 0.85 | 0.1648 | 1.6691 | 0.0988 |
| 1.00 | **0.1743** | **1.1630** | **0.1498** |

Realized **speed rises with c_cmd** — the same ordering as `E[vx | c]`. The policy obeys the
command it actually learned. Because it answers "go faster" by **lengthening the stride**
(0.0795 → 0.1498) rather than quickening, cadence *falls*. **That is the inverted slope, fully
accounted for:** it is not a sign error in the reward, it is the actor executing obs53's true
marginal meaning in this dataset.

## 3. Reward / advantage attribution — no optimizer step

Bounded **no-update** rollout through the real trainer against the failed checkpoint
(`--dry-proof-out`; **no `ppo_*.zip` written**), plus the pre-run CY proof and every `[regime]`
line of the real 884,736-step run. Comparison at **κ = 0**, the only geometry carrying all three
cadences.

| stratum | c | samples | obs53 | adv_mu CY | adv_sd CY | adv_mu 884k | adv_sd 884k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `acq_kap0_c0.70` | 0.70 | 14 784 | 0.350 | 1.3348 | 1.2939 | 1.6820 | 1.8693 |
| `acq_kap0_c0.85` | 0.85 | 22 208 | 0.425 | 1.4231 | 1.2925 | 1.8232 | 1.9036 |
| `acq_kap0_c1.00` | 1.00 | 22 144 | 0.500 | 1.4598 | 1.2839 | 1.9070 | 1.9158 |

| | CY | 884k |
|---|---|---|
| between-cadence spread of `adv_mu` | 0.1250 | 0.2250 |
| mean within-stratum `adv_sd` | 1.2901 | 1.8962 |
| **separation ratio** | **0.097** | **0.119** |

**The command-specific advantage signal is ~10–12 % of the within-stratum noise.** Across the
real run's own iterations the ratio was 0.286 / 0.010 / 0.286 — same order.

**And the ordering is monotone in cadence** (`adv_mu` rises 0.70 → 0.85 → 1.00 in the 884k proof
and in 2 of 3 logged iterations). Monotone means the signal prefers **one end of the cadence axis
everywhere** — a global push, not per-command targets. That is precisely the gradient that
produces a uniform cadence shift.

⚠ This run used `--adv-norm-command-regime`, which normalises advantages **within** each stratum
and therefore removes these between-stratum mean offsets from the gradient entirely. Command
conditioning had to come from *within-regime* variation — and with c predictable from vx (§2),
there is no within-regime structure that requires reading obs53.

## 4. Counterfactual reward — the term itself is correctly shaped

Identical touchdown traces scored under each command with the **actual** trainer method.

| c_cmd | argmin of the penalty | penalty at optimum |
|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | **0.70 Hz** | 0.00002 |
| 0.85 | **0.85 Hz** | 0.00002 |
| 1.00 | **1.00 Hz** | 0.00000 |

Cross-score (rows = true gait Hz, columns = command used to score), ×19.5:

| true \ cmd | 0.70 | 0.85 | 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.70** | **0.0003** | 0.3095 | 1.0180 |
| **0.85** | 0.2254 | **0.0003** | 0.2254 |
| **1.00** | 0.6665 | 0.1714 | **0.0000** |

Diagonal 0.0002, off-diagonal 0.4360 → **separation 0.4358** = **19.4 % of one `track_feet`**,
1.82 % of the positive per-step sum. **`COMMAND-SPECIFIC OPTIMA EXIST`** and are exact.

**So the reward is not the problem.** It has distinct, correctly-placed optima with real
separation. At the operating points the checkpoints actually occupy, however, the cheapest command
for the realized gait is `c = 1.00` in **every** cell for **both** checkpoints — the gait is too
fast for every command, and c=1.00 is merely the least-wrong.

## Why not the other two verdicts

**Not `CADENCE REPRESENTATION BOTTLENECK`** — that verdict requires *"training coverage is
identifiable"*, and §2 shows it is not (2 of 18 cells; c=0.70 identifiable nowhere). It also
requires the actor to be *"effectively insensitive/non-distinct"*; the actor moves 30 % of its
action norm on an obs53 sweep, so "insensitive" is false on the measurement.

**Not `CADENCE TRAINING-SIGNAL BOTTLENECK`** — that verdict requires the actor to *"respond
distinctly to obs53"*. At `cos(cadence, speed) = +0.87…+0.91` the response is not distinct from
the speed channel. The weak advantage separation in §3 is real, but it is **downstream** of the
confound: when c is predictable from vx, no per-command advantage structure can form in the first
place. Treating it as the root cause would send the next repair at the optimiser instead of the
dataset.

**`CADENCE STRATA CONFOUND` is the only verdict consistent with all four measurements**, and it
is the only one that predicts the *sign* of the observed inversion.
