## 2 · The §3 premise that fails: stride is not observational

This is the item I most need you to see before authorizing, because it governs what the eventual report may claim.

`env.track_add_cfg` is set **unconditionally** (trainer `:5035-5039`), so the reward takes the `_track_additive` branch (`:2176-2187`). That term pays `w = {lat 3, roll 2, rest 2, jnt 5, feet 4}`, Σ 16 — it *is* the imitation income. Three groups constrain stride geometry:

| group | w | σ | what it prices |
|---|---|---|---|
| `track_feet` | 4.0 | 60 mm | foot world-XY minus pelvis XY, rotated into the pelvis-yaw frame, per phase against `rf_rel`/`lf_rel` — the fore-aft component **is step length** |
| `track_jnt` | 5.0 | 10° | all 12 joints vs `jq[phase]` — hip-pitch swing amplitude is the kinematic determinant of stride |
| `track_rest` | 2.0 | 60 mm (x) | pelvis path-frame x vs the reference's **cumulative-x** schedule, anchor advancing `loop_xadv` per cycle — cumulative under-striding accrues without bound |

These targets are stride-specific, not generic: reference fore-aft foot excursion scales monotonically with designed stride across the family (0.0765 m at stride 0.118 → 0.1937 m at stride 0.379), which against σ 60 mm is a live gradient toward each member's own stride geometry.

Independently, `r_fwd` (speed, w 12) and `cadence_track` (cadence, w 19.5) **jointly** determine stride = v/c. So even setting the imitation term aside, "stride is free to move" does not follow from deleting the stride-named terms.

I did **not** attempt to remove or gate the imitation term — that would be exactly the "new reward architecture" your §4 forbids, and it is your call, not mine. My recommendation is to run the unit as built and describe stride as *unshaped by any stride-named term*, which is true and provable, rather than *unconstrained*, which is not.
