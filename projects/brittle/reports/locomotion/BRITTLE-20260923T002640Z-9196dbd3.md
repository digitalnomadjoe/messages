Same code, same cell family, heading +x from the start: 0 falls in a 14 s headless probe (`sessions/headx_test01`) and then the two full sessions (§7). Log: `sessions/negcontrol_heading_minus_y_showroom_20260922T231339Z_r1/demo.log` (both halves). Candidate mechanism, read in `rl/isaac_parity/runtime_port.py::obs54`: `base_angvel = R.T @ w_body` where `w_body` is already the body-frame gyro (`isaac_plant.read_state` does `R.T @ w_world`) — the comment calls it the "legacy double-rotated channel (obs contract)"; the net channel is `R(−ψ)·w_body` with ψ the world yaw, so relative to the training frame (ψ = 90°) a −y heading swaps the roll/pitch rate slots (a 90° rotation of the rate vector), −x is the opposite rotation and +y would be the exact negation. The −x tail walked fall-free, so the rotation alone does not explain the failure; what is established is the heading DEPENDENCE, not the mechanism. Every other channel is body-frame (projected gravity, foot shifts `R.T @ (foot − pelvis)`, FK leg odometry). This is a property of the frozen Crown's obs contract (the port replicates it bit-exactly and must not be "fixed"); DEMO-01's ±26–29° turns are the tolerated envelope. **Recommended rule (owner decision):** every Crown deployment spawns heading +x; demo paths run along +x — applied in this unit.

## 5. THIRD_PERSON_FOLLOW camera (ticket §4/§5) — `rl/demo01/third_person_camera.py`

**5.1 Design.** Only the camera trajectory is filtered; robot state and policy output are never touched (the live loop only reads state for the camera; the renders replay recorded commands). Target position = the pelvis position through a 2-stage first-order cascade (the DEMO-01 world-referenced smoothed target, refined from one 0.6 s stage to two 0.45 s stages); heading = the pelvis yaw, unwrapped, through a 2-stage 0.9 s cascade — the camera is not rigidly attached to the pelvis orientation (roll and pitch are never used, yaw is heavily filtered); vertical datum = smoothed pelvis z − 0.3373 (a smoothed local-ground estimate that rides slopes without step changes); eye = target − 1.4 m along the smoothed heading, 0.65 m above the datum; look-at = target + 1.3 m ahead, 0.15 m above the datum ⇒ robot lower-middle, terrain ahead in the upper half; eye z raised to ground(eye) + 0.25 m when the trailing terrain rises (ray-cast). Live: one `set_camera_view` per rendered policy step (50 Hz; r2 receipt `set_camera_view_calls {'free': 1, 'third_person': 7250}` = one per step) on Kit's default perspective viewport camera; offscreen: advanced every 2 ms physics substep, frames captured at 60 fps by a Camera sensor with focal 30 mm on a 36 mm aperture (the focal applies to the sensor only). FREE (`--cam free` / [X]) = operator owns the viewport after one framing, zero writes — kept as the debugging option; no fixed world-anchored mode exists.
