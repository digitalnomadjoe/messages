## 1 · The rung is reachable inside existing geometry

Same-foot speed decomposes exactly as stride × cadence, and the measured numbers close:

| | stride (m) | cadence (Hz) | TRUE speed (m/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DR06G-884736 today, cmd 0.272219, legacy law | 0.3334 | 0.7710 | **0.2571** (0.3334 × 0.7710 = 0.2571 ✓) |
| same policy **at the banked member's own stride** | **0.3793** | 0.7710 | **0.2924** |
| for exactly 0.3000 at unchanged cadence | 0.3891 | 0.7710 | 0.3000 |

The member's geometry (0.3793 m) delivers **0.2924** with cadence untouched — a materially new basin over the 0.257 ceiling, reached purely by stride. Exactly 0.3000 would need 0.3891 m, marginally *beyond* the reference's own stride; your tolerance on the target means that distinction does not decide the unit.

## 3 · Decision, per your §6

Your stop condition names this outcome: *"Do not add another reward term automatically.
