S2 discounted returns are measured per world from the recorded reward series; the training-regime discounted anchor (1,108) is inferred from the ledger + history stats.
- **S6 is synthetic** — declared assumptions hold r_fwd/r_zt/track at policy values; a real long-stride policy would differ in every channel.
- **S4/S5 horizons are assumptions** (1 s / 4.5 s); their rows scale linearly with the assumed horizon and cannot cross the S2–S1 gap for any horizon.
- Bounded channels: S1 `p_flat`+`base` ∈ [−0.09, +0.04]; S6 `p_slip`/stomp/foot_lift as tabled. **No ceiling value was silently substituted for a measurement** — S3's track 5.959 and S1b's 2.070/16.000 are computed, labeled constructions.
- The anchor LSQ fit in the track validation identifies the spawn anchor to ~mm; residual 1.3e-04 max. `r_zt`/`p_slip`/`foot_lift` validations are anchor-free.
- D-27/D-28 magnitudes are specific to this reference's baked +90° yaw and sway geometry; the defects themselves are structural (any yaw ≠ 0 / any swaying reference).
- Discounted rows use γ=0.99 (provenance §1). `d_act_rate`/`d_act_acc` are excluded from books per the repo's own ledger contract (`:2557`).
