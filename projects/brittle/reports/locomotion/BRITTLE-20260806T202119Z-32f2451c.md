## 0. Headline

**PPO held everything it was given and closed one of the two gates — but never both at once, and the reason is measurable.**

- **Robustness is untouched: 0/16 falls in all six cells at every one of the eight checkpoints.** The Q2 achievement survives the whole run.
- **The right gate closes.** Right stochastic κ_net crosses −1.0 at checkpoint 774,144 and stays there for the rest of the run: −1.074, −1.020, −1.024, −1.020, −1.034, −1.043 — **6 of 8 checkpoints meet it.**
- **The left gate never closes — and moves away.** Left stochastic κ_net goes +0.988 (Q2) → +0.914 → +0.941 → +0.775 … +0.833. **0 of 8 checkpoints meet it.**
- ★★★ **The two turns anti-correlate across the ladder: Pearson(left κ, |right κ|) = −0.891.** Every checkpoint that strengthens the right turn weakens the left by a comparable amount. This is not noise and not a tuning accident — it is one shared-trunk MLP trading one turn against the other under a single policy-gradient objective.

**Best checkpoint 258,048 (`24c47d8e45815243`) passes 11 of 14 gates.** Deterministic curvature actually improved on the right (−1.044 → −1.075) and held on the left (+1.102 → +1.066, −3.3%); straight retention tightened (|κ| ≤ 0.010). The three misses are both stochastic κ gates and a small torque-burst worsening. **No hard stop was triggered.**

## 7. What this measures

Q2 established that imitation has no signal where the student dies. Q3 shows PPO *does* supply that signal — it closed the right gate and held zero falls across two million steps — but that **a single shared-trunk MLP under one policy-gradient objective cannot hold both turn magnitudes at once on this manifold**. The −0.891 anti-correlation is the finding: capacity or representation, not robustness and not reward, is now the binding constraint. Both turns remain correctly classified, correctly signed, fall-free and within 3 % of Q2 deterministically — the deficit is confined to stochastic magnitude, and it moves between the two sides rather than shrinking.

Since the enum's `THREE-SKILL STUDENT READY FOR VISUAL REVIEW` requires a checkpoint that passes every gate and none does, C6 forbids returning it, and no checkpoint was staged for visual review. `PPO REGRESSED ACQUIRED SKILLS` is false on every measured axis (falls, classification, sign, deterministic curvature, straight retention all held or improved). `GUARD REGISTRATION FAILED` and `MULTIPLE BLOCKERS` are false — the window opened, registered and closed cleanly.

**For your gate, three options I can distinguish with evidence — I recommend the first:** (i) a **representation/capacity ticket** — the anti-correlation is a shared-trunk symptom and the natural probes are per-command output heads or a wider trunk, held to the same fourteen gates; (ii) accept the ±0.09 stochastic-magnitude deficit as a spec question and re-cut the gate at the measured achievable band, since every other property is met and deterministic curvature exceeds Q2; (iii) a second bounded seed to test whether the anti-correlation is seed-specific — cheap (24 s of GPU) but it does not address a structural cause. Nothing is banked, promoted or pointed at.
