So the commanded sit is a *kneel-sit*: feet 57–157 mm in front of the hip, knees folded behind, and the weight must be carried by the thigh-ends (knee region) plus the feet — the soles alone cannot hold it (CoM 51 mm behind their rear row). (Rotating the body about the knee-end contact line to bring the lifted soles down lands the *toe* row first — it is farthest from that line — which is exactly the settled support found in §8.1.) The pelvis never reaches the floor. On the box envelopes the thigh-end interferes with the floor by 9.6 mm at soles-flat; on the real meshes it clears by 8.3 mm (the envelope is 18 mm deeper than the part).

### 4.2 Along the naive path the CoM leaves the soles at a third of the fold

Linear hip/knee blend from the DS to the target with the ankle slaved (foot flat, pelvis level): the CoM crosses behind the heel row at **s ≈ 0.33–0.35 (knee ≈ −106..−108°)** while the hip is still 283–293 mm up and the CoM 213–220 mm up; the thigh-ends are ~170 mm up. Everything after that point is a backward tip about the heel row (ticket §3's "support transfer" is a fall unless something is done).

### 4.3 The lever that exists — a nose-down pelvis lean through the ankle — and its wall

Commanding ankle = −(hip+knee) − L pitches the pelvis nose-down by L with the soles still flat, which moves the CoM forward over the soles. Kinematic field (hip × knee × lean, `lean_study`): at knee −155° the CoM is back over the soles only for L ≥ 50° (hip 60) / 40° (hip 70). But leaning forward LIFTS the knee-ends (they are behind the hip): thigh-end height at knee −155 is −10 mm at L 0, +32 at L 10, +75 at L 20, +118 at L 30, +121 at L 40, +123 at L 50. An exhaustive search over hip 40..90 × knee −155..−100 × lean 0..60 with both sole margins ≥ 5 mm gives a **minimum thigh-end height of 115 mm** (hip 90, knee −155, lean 60) and 120–125 mm for any moderate lean. **There is no configuration with the CoM over the soles and the knee-ends near the floor**: the transfer from soles to thigh-ends is necessarily a gravity tip from ≥ 115 mm. (With the pelvis level the CoM is behind the heel row for every knee ≤ −108° at every hip 40..75°.) Once the CoM is behind the heel row no foot contact can restore it (a toe push adds nose-up moment), so the only design freedom is *where* the tip starts (lower CoM, knee-ends lower) and *how gently* (start at zero velocity at the margin).

### 4.4 Self-contact at the target

`data/self_contact_onset.json` (hip 60, foot flat): the v2 envelopes `col_{side}_hip_roll_link` vs `col_{side}_knee_pitch_link` begin to interpenetrate at **knee −143°** (−2.6 mm at −144, −7.9 at −148, **−10.5 mm at ≤ −150**). The real mesh convex hulls (DEV plant, hulls compiled) begin at **knee −148°** (−0.2 mm), −2.65 at −150, −5.1 at −152, **−8.4 mm at −155**. The commanded knee is inside the self-collision zone of BOTH representations; the plant's knee ROM (−155°) is beyond the point where the shank hull enters the hip-roll bracket hull. (Whether the real parts nest in a way a convex hull cannot represent is a CAD question — flagged, not decided.)
