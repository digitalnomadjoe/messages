## 4. ⚠ The action budget is **not** the binding constraint — correcting my own earlier arithmetic

Earlier in this ticket I computed, from `Q_DEFAULT` and `ACTION_SCALE` alone, that all six sagittal
joints demand **1.08×–1.39×** the `q_des` amplitude cap (`2·action_scale`), and that the left knee's
reference *mean* would need |a| = 1.19. **That arithmetic is correct and it still stands as an upper
bound: the reference is not expressible by this setpoint law at any action.** That is a live C5
finding.

**But it is not what is limiting the policy today**, and I should not have led with it:

| joint | cap (2·scale) | q_des P2P used | **cap used** | \|a\| p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hip_pitch | 0.30 | 0.058 | **19 %** | 0.31 |
| knee | 0.40 | 0.155 | **39 %** | 0.39 |
| ank_pitch | 0.30 | 0.022 | **7 %** | 0.08 |

The policy uses **7–39 %** of the budget it already has. Raising `action_scale` would change
nothing — there is no saturation to relieve. The reference has no authority not because the budget
is too small, but because **there is no channel from the reference to the setpoint at all.**

## 5. Recommendation — exactly one

**Restore the canonical setpoint law for the sagittal chain: change the `q_des` base from the
constant `Q_DEFAULT` to the phase-indexed `q_ref`.**

```python
# rl/envs/mini_biped_yaw_cmd_env.py:761
- q_des = Q_DEFAULT + self.action_scale * applied
+ q_des = q_ref_now + self.action_scale * applied
```

This is one line, and it is not an invention — it is the law already written in `CLAUDE.md` under
**Canonical Low-Level Control Law** (`q_des = active q_ref + action_scale * policy_action`), from
which this env silently deviates. It is the smallest change that gives the reference any authority
over the realized gait, and it simultaneously dissolves the C5 cap finding in §4: once the setpoint
is centred on the reference, the ±0.15/±0.20 residual budget only has to cover *deviation* from the
reference, not the reference's full amplitude and mean offset.

⚠ **This is a canonical mutation and it invalidates every checkpoint in this lane** — the policy
would be optimising a different setpoint law and must be retrained from scratch. It requires Joe's
authorization and a Guard canonical-mutation decision. **I have not made this change.**
