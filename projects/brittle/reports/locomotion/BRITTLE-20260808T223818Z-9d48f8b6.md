## Q1 — speed: monotone, compressed, and not at fixed cadence

| `c_cmd` | d(realized speed)/d(`v_cmd`) | cadence spread over the sweep |
|---|---|---|
| 0.700 | +0.135 | 0.220 Hz |
| 0.850 | +0.150 | 0.699 Hz |
| 0.925 | +0.231 | 0.567 Hz |
| 1.000 | **+0.418** | 0.602 Hz |

Ideal slope is ~1.0. At `c=1.000`, realized/commanded walks from **1.282** at `v_cmd=0.118` down
to **0.838** at `v_cmd=0.240` — the policy pulls every command toward one preferred speed.
Across the whole straight surface realized speed spans only **0.134–0.201 m/s** while the command
spans **0.107–0.240**. And cadence is not held: it falls by up to 0.70 Hz across a single
constant-`c_cmd` speed sweep.

## Q2 — cadence: inverted, in every matched-speed contrast

| matched speed | d(cadence)/d(`c_cmd`) | d(stride)/d(`c_cmd`) |
|---|---|---|
| ~0.12 m/s | **−2.123** | **+0.135** |
| ~0.15 m/s | **−3.308** | **+0.424** |
| ~0.17 m/s | **−2.819** | **+0.305** |
| ~0.20 m/s | **−2.174** | **+0.525** |

Both signs are backwards from the specification: **raising commanded cadence lowers realized
cadence and lengthens stride.** The worst cells are the low-cadence ones — at `c_cmd=0.700` the
robot runs at **~2.0 Hz**, a realized/commanded ratio of **2.9**.

## Q3 — the two commands are not the two axes

```
realized_speed   = +0.289*v_cmd  +0.069*c_cmd  +0.048
realized_cadence = −5.808*v_cmd  −2.414*c_cmd  +4.449
```

The cadence output answers **more strongly to the speed command (−5.81) than to the cadence
command (−2.41)**. Stride does span 0.065–0.269 m (4×), so this is not one frozen gait — the
surface has real internal variation. It is simply not parameterised by the two knobs it is
labelled with.

## Q4 — curvature works

36/36 survival. Sign agreement **12/12**; `corr(κ_cmd, realized) = +0.885`; bilateral asymmetry
≤ 0.014 everywhere. Magnitude is compressed at the extremes (`κ=+2.00 → +0.66`,
`κ=−2.00 → −0.77`) but ordering and sign are correct throughout. **Curvature is the one command
dimension that behaves as labelled.**

## Q5 — the 100 % young-death was a population artifact

**90/90 episodes (100 %) survived to the 8 s horizon**, zero early terminations, under
deterministic nominal fresh starts. Training-time `young_death_pct` was 100.0 at every
checkpoint. Both are true of their own populations: the training figure is measured over 2304
randomised DR worlds with pushes and the full command-strata curriculum; this is the canonical
nominal fresh-start evaluator. **The checkpoint does not fail fresh spawn.**

## The control — why the inversion is not my measurement

The straight sweep drives every cell from **one fixed phase table**, varying only the command.
That is legitimate because the observation is `[base50, sin φ, cos φ, vx_cmd, cadence_cmd/2]`
(`isaac_spectator.py:524-526`) — **no reference geometry enters obs**; the table contributes only
loop length and the yaw profile. But "legitimate by reading the code" is not proof, so three
trained cells were re-run on their **own native tables**:

| cell | own table | fixed table | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vx 0.1301 / c 0.85` | 1.7065 Hz | 1.7074 Hz | 0.0009 |
| `vx 0.1003 / c 0.85` | 1.8714 Hz | 1.8753 Hz | 0.0039 |
| `vx 0.1071 / c 0.70` | 2.0533 Hz | 2.0533 Hz | 0.0000 |

**The table is immaterial.** Two further guards: `step_rate/cadence = 2.000 ± 0.02` in **all 18**
straight cells with asymmetry ≤ 0.033, so the gait is genuinely alternating and the cadence
figures are not contact chatter; and a 120-step pilot produced incoherent cadences that the
400-step runs did not — **a short window makes this estimator lie**, which is why every number
here is from 400 steps.

## What this means for the stride complaint

Joe's "stride shorter than desired" is measured: the visually-passed cell (`vx 0.153, c 1.000`)
realizes **0.148 m**. The surface's longest stride is **0.269 m** at (`vx 0.240, c 1.000`),
against a constructed `stride_max` of 0.28. **Longer stride is already reachable** — but by
commanding more speed at `c=1.0`, not by the cadence knob, and the cadence knob currently moves
stride the wrong way. Chasing stride with gait polish before the cadence axis is fixed would tune
against an inverted control.

**No gait-polish target is recommended: the checkpoint is NOT QUALIFIED.** The next work is the
cadence command path — not the reward, not the reference, and not the gait.
