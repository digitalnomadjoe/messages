(kp=1.00, kd nominal, no delay/other perturbation); 20% LOW-SIDE support
kp ~ U(0.70, 1.00); 50% HIGH-SIDE target kp ~ U(1.00, 1.20). Rationale: DR02

## Pass criteria
A checkpoint qualifies only if ALL of: (A) nominal retention inside the frozen
Crown acceptance envelope; (B) low-side retention — no new CPU regression vs

required; continue at least one more checkpoint after first qualification
within the fixed budget; no early-checkpoint rescue. Cross-engine rule: a
policy that improves Isaac but regresses MuJoCo is FAIL; robustness that
